About The Tesla Dispatch

Weekday briefing on Tesla's cars, autonomy, robots, energy and stock.

Colophon

The Tesla Dispatch is an independent weekday digest of public news, X posts, regulatory filings and market data about Tesla, Inc., and is not affiliated with the company. Quotes are verbatim; emoji in quoted posts are omitted for print. Market data are delayed and may be revised. Not investment advice.

Editions

Weekday Edition. Each issue is published on this site and as a printable PDF, linked from the issue page. Past issues are in the archive, and new issues appear in the Atom feed.

Typography

Set in Cinzel Decorative (masthead), Playfair Display (headlines), Source Serif Pro (text) and Libre Franklin (labels and tables).
