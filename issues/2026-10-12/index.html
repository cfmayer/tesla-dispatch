The Morning Picture

300 Cybercabs in Austin and "Dallas soon," a new FSD build, and a week of Paris and the Roadster ahead

Tesla's Robotaxi account said on Saturday that more than 300 Cybercabs now serve Austin, eight times as many as a month ago, and Tesla's head of AI software, Ashok Elluswamy, added "Dallas soon." An independent tracker counted 105 Cybercabs in unsupervised service the same day. Software 2026.38.5, carrying Full Self-Driving (FSD) (Supervised) v14.3.12 for newer cars and v14.3 Lite for Hardware 3, began reaching owners on Saturday. Slovakia is still preparing the letter that would make it the ninth European Union (EU) country to allow the system.

Speaking by video at the fan-run X Takeover at Giga Texas, Elon Musk said Tesla aims to produce ten chip designs a year and that China's magnet export ban set Optimus back two to three months. The stock rose 2.05% to $382.70 on Friday, ahead of the S&P 500's 0.59% gain. The Paris Motor Show runs through Sunday, the new Roadster is unveiled Thursday, and third-quarter results follow on October 21. Tesla has filed nothing new with the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) since Friday morning.

I · Full Self-Driving (FSD)

Cybercabs "everywhere in Austin," with Dallas next

Tesla published its first official Cybercab fleet number on the two-year anniversary of the We, Robot event. Independent counts are lower, and the Dallas date is open.

Robotaxi: 300+ by Tesla's count

"One month after launch, we've scaled to 300+ Cybercabs serving Austin – an 8x increase in 30 days," Tesla's Robotaxi account posted on Saturday, and Tesla's main account amplified it within minutes, TeslaNorth reported. It is the first official count of Cybercabs in service. Ashok Elluswamy quote-posted it:

"Two years since the 10/10 Cybercab WeRobot event, the cabs are everywhere in Austin now. Dallas soon."
Ashok Elluswamy · @aelluswamy · Saturday, via Teslarati

It is the first time Tesla has named the next city for public Cybercab rides, Teslarati noted; Dallas riders still get Model Ys for now, and no date was given. Public Cybercab rides began in Austin on September 4.

Registrations and service are different numbers. The Texas automated-vehicle registry lists 319 Cybercabs after last Thursday's 150-car batch, while RobotaxiTracker, which counts vehicles from sightings and state records, listed 275 Tesla robotaxis in unsupervised service in Austin on Saturday, 105 of them Cybercabs, The Inference reported. Tesla has not said how many of the 300 carry a safety monitor. The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) expects Tesla's answers on how the Cybercab was certified by October 30.

Software: 2026.38.5 with v14.3.12

Tesla began rolling out software 2026.38.5 on Saturday, October 10, with FSD (Supervised) v14.3.12 for AI4 (Hardware 4) cars and v14.3 Lite for Hardware 3 cars, according to Not a Tesla App's tracker, which showed it on 12 cars tracked by TeslaFi, about 0.1% of that fleet, by Monday morning. The release notes carry over the 2026.38 features: Halloween Mode, Emergency Drive Away, Supercharger site maps and Automatic Collision Evasion. The previous build, 2026.33.5 with v14.3.11, reached early testers last week.

Europe: Slovakia's letter, Paris test drives

Slovak Transport Minister Jozef Ráž said his ministry will send the Netherlands a letter recognizing the Dutch vehicle authority's (RDW) approval "in the coming days," TeslAnt reported; it had not been sent as of the weekend, and Tesla has given no Slovak launch date. "Just be careful — you are still the driver, and you are responsible for the car," Ráž said. The EU's Technical Committee on Motor Vehicles (TCMV), which could approve the system bloc-wide, did not vote on October 6 and next meets in December.

At the Paris Motor Show (Mondial de l'Auto), which runs October 12 to 18, Tesla will show a Cybercab from its US fleet and offer free FSD (Supervised) test drives in six cars; European drivers have logged more than 100 million km on the system, according to Tesla, Drive Tesla reported.

Quick Hit

Waymo will soon extend robotaxi testing onto Phoenix freeways for the first time, Drive Tesla noted. Waymo has 1,154 autonomous vehicles authorized in Texas, per The Inference.

II · Sales & Products

A Roadster week, and a feature that came and went in Canada

The new Roadster is unveiled Thursday; Tesla's Canadian site briefly listed vehicle-to-load power on two Model Y versions.

Roadster on Thursday

Tesla unveils the new Roadster on Thursday, October 15, at 9 p.m. EDT, Tesla Mag reported. Musk, asked about it at X Takeover, called it "a little event next week," according to the interview transcript.

Canada: V2L on, then off

Tesla's Canadian website added vehicle-to-load (V2L, 120V 20A through an outlet adapter, up to 2.4 kW) to the Model Y Premium all-wheel drive and Performance on Thursday evening, then removed it from both by Friday afternoon, Drive Tesla reported. Tesla has not explained the change; V2L reached the Model Y in the US and China over the past week. Every Canadian Model 3 trim now has the 16-inch touchscreen and a black headliner, Drive Tesla added.

III · Chips, Robots & AI

Ten chip designs a year, and an Optimus that plays Diablo

At X Takeover, Musk described a fast-iteration fab at Giga Texas and a magnet-driven delay for Optimus. Tesla's AI team now posts as @TeslaSI.

X Takeover at Giga Texas

In a 41-minute video interview with Tesla Owners Silicon Valley on Saturday, Musk said Tesla plans to make ten chip designs a year at Giga Texas, against an industry standard of about one every two years; that China's magnet export ban set Optimus back two to three months, prompting plans for a domestic supply chain; and that he wants to avoid any robotaxi accident because of the worldwide headlines and the risk of a regulatory shutdown, according to the CEOInterviews summary. He said he still has "a lot to learn about chip making" and that Terafab will start on conventional equipment, All Things Elon reported. Of rivals' robot demo videos, he said real AI means showing a robot something it has never seen and watching it still work, Tesla Mag reported.

Digital Optimus

Replying to a post about a Tesla job listing for Digital Optimus's "Gaming Track," Musk wrote early Saturday, per Drive Tesla:

"We're making interesting progress here. Digital Optimus can play about halfway through the Diablo campaign so far just by looking at the screen like a human. Skill at Counter-Strike and other fast games is good too. League is also in training. Goal is to generalize across all games."
Elon Musk · @elonmusk · Saturday, via Drive Tesla

The listing says the team will work on the agent's memory, planning and real-time decisions and help bring such agents into FSD and the physical Optimus robot. Digital Optimus, announced in March with xAI, works software through the screen with keyboard and mouse inputs.

Quick Hit

Grok Bot shopping. Musk said users can photograph a credit card and Grok Bot will "scour the Internet for the best deal" and order the item; he said it bought LEGO for him, TeslaNorth reported.
