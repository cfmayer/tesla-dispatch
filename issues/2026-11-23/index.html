The Morning Picture

FSD v15 reaches its first owners, with a car-drawn view of the road

Tesla began rolling out FSD (Supervised) v15 on Saturday afternoon, claiming "25x better handling of safety critical cases" and debuting Tesla Reality View, a generated overhead picture of the drive that shows where the car plans to turn and park. Elon Musk said the wide release follows next month, that Tesla's next chip, AI5, will go to the Optimus robot while cars stay on AI4, and that built-in Starlink for every model could come toward the end of next year.

Tesla Charging posted 20 new Supercharger sites with 238 stalls last week, plus a Megacharger in Mesquite, Texas; Tesla said it will fund Quebec's $2,000 EV rebate after the province's program ends; and Cybertrucks from model year 2027 turn tighter than a Model Y. TSLA fell 7.6% to $345.82 on Friday as the S&P 500 dropped 3.7% and HSBC moved the stock to Hold from Reduce.

I · Full Self-Driving (FSD)

FSD v15 starts rolling out

A new vision encoder shared with Robotaxi and Optimus, a 25-fold gain in Tesla's critical-intervention measure and a visualization drawn by the driving network itself.

"FSD v15 now starting to roll out – upgrading every part of the end-to-end driving network," Tesla posted at 2:24 p.m. EST Saturday. The post promised "25x better handling of safety critical cases," Tesla Reality View, "a live visualization of the drive that lets you see & control what FSD is planning to do," and navigation for the start and end of a trip that "now learns end-to-end from fleet route preferences"; later v15 releases will extend that to the whole route. "FSD v15 releasing to the first wave of customers today!!" wrote Ashok Elluswamy, who leads Tesla's AI software (post).

The release notes, posted by the Tesla Super Intelligence account, say the upgraded vision encoder "shared between FSD, Robotaxi, and Optimus" is "trained on millions of internet videos for improved generalization and data efficiency, improving the critical intervention rate by 25x." They also list better detection of road signs, small animals, debris, chains, potholes and deep standing water; speed handling for school zones and bad weather; parking spots chosen from a driver's own history; and attention monitoring that works with hats and sunglasses. The same software, build 2026.38.6.11, adds Speed Camera Slowdown and a Bike Rack Mode, according to Not a Tesla App. Tesla still gives only the multiplier, not the underlying intervention rate, Electrek noted.

The first wave went to members of Tesla's Early Access Program, Teslarati reported. By Sunday the tracker Teslascope had logged v15.0.1 on 11 cars, all new Model Y vehicles with AI4 hardware in the United States, Volts & Bots reported. Musk had written at 2:30 a.m. EST Saturday: "FSD V15 goes to wide release next month. It will be something special."

Reality View, drawn by the car

"V15 debuts the Tesla Reality View. The same end-to-end driving model also renders a realistic view of the world around the vehicle," Elluswamy wrote. "It's not there just because it looks cool, but it also communicates the intent of the car. The arrows show the turns FSD intents to take, the pins show its intent to park in a spot and so on." Comparing the old and new screens, he added: "The crazier thing is that the right side uses less compute than what the left used to take." Owners can tap a point in the view to send the car there, Musk said.

On Sunday Musk described the machinery: the car forks off the Tesla Universal Vision Encoder features that also feed "the 3B encoder model that produces the driving actions," then runs "a separate ~1B diffusion model" that turns the previous frame plus noise into the current one, producing "8 images at 36 fps." It runs on "half a TRIP out of three on SoC B," one of the AI4 computer's accelerators, he wrote, and "the latency is a bit high due to compute constraints," with ways to cut it by about 35%.

Quick Hit

Around the branch. "FSD v15 navigating around a tree in the road," Tesla posted at 11:40 a.m. EST Sunday, sharing an owner's clip of the car steering around a large branch blocking a road.

"25x safer than v14 across the fleet"

"v15 is 25x safer than v14 across the fleet. That's hard to notice on any individual drive because most drives are already very safe. The difference only becomes clear when you aggregate millions of miles," Phil Duan, who works on the Tesla AI team, wrote at 5:17 p.m. EST Saturday. Musk added on Sunday: "Some people thought we meant 25% improvement, no we mean 25 times!"

Cars stay on AI4

Asked when AI5 would reach new cars, Musk replied: "It's going to Optimus. Cars will remain at AI4. That's enough to solve superhuman driving." He called the coming AI4.5 board "a relatively small performance improvement that is mostly due to needing to upgrade RAM to LPDDR5X" (post) and said Tesla cut its memory from 32GB to 24GB because "RAM is super expensive" (post). "AI4 costs twice as much now due to memory and storage price hikes," he wrote. Tesla has not said which cars get AI4.5 or when, and v15 runs on AI4, TeslaNorth noted.

Starlink built in, except in China

Asked about an embedded Starlink option "in all models (beyond the Cybercab)," Musk answered at 8:15 p.m. EST Sunday: "Hopefully available towards the end of next year. This will be important for unsupervised FSD, so passengers have high bandwidth everywhere for work and entertainment." He added: "Except in China, as Starlink is illegal there."

II · Sales & Products

Tesla will pay Quebec's $2,000 rebate itself

The province's Roulez vert rebate ends with 2026; Tesla says it will take the same amount off eligible cars delivered from Jan 1. The 2027 Cybertruck gets a tighter turn.

Quebec's Roulez vert rebate of up to $2,000 CAD for a new electric car applies only to vehicles delivered and registered by Dec 31. For qualifying Teslas delivered on or after Jan 1, 2027, Tesla says it will take up to $2,000 CAD off the price after taxes while its offer lasts, with no end date posted, TeslaNorth reported. The car must have a base price below $65,000 CAD and appear on Quebec's eligible list. The discount can be combined with the federal Electric Vehicle Affordability Program, worth up to $4,000 CAD for new assessments from Jan 1, though Tesla says the Model 3 does not currently qualify for the federal program. Quebec's rebate fell from $7,000 CAD in 2024 to $4,000 CAD in 2025 and $2,000 CAD in 2026, and the province adds a $125 CAD annual registration fee for electric cars next year.

A tighter-turning Cybertruck

"It's not the size ... it's how you maneuver it. Even tighter turns now rolling out to new Cybertrucks," the Cybertruck account posted at 1:43 a.m. EST Saturday. Wes Morrill, Tesla's Cybertruck lead engineer, explained: "We modified the steering rack for model year '27 to have more travel - making the turning radius better than the Model Y." He said it is "a hardware change that cut over with model year 2027."

Cybercab "would win even at price parity"

Responding to a report that some Dallas riders prefer the Cybercab to discounted rival robotaxis, Musk wrote at 4:44 p.m. EST Sunday: "Most people don't yet realize that Cybercab would win even at price parity and its cost will be much less." Tesla's Robotaxi account shared a clip from downtown Austin of a Cybercab stopping while a human driver kept going: "Looking in every direction at once, at all times."

Quick Hit

Optimus. "Optimus is awesome. It will be our finest product ever and (I think) the best product of all time of any kind," Musk wrote on Sunday, replying to a post about the vision encoder FSD v15 shares with the robot.

III · Energy & Charging

Charger pins that cluster, and a grid-talking site

Software 2026.44.1 groups chargers on the map, a California Supercharger opened faster by talking to its utility, and 141 Wall Connector for Business sites went live.

"Vehicle touchscreen improvement in latest software update (2026.44.1): charger pins on the map now cluster, showing network coverage as you zoom out," Tesla Charging posted at 2:38 p.m. EST Wednesday, adding that more third-party chargers have been added in Europe.

"Our Cloverdale, CA Supercharger is a PG&E Flex Connect site, talking to the grid over IEEE 2030.5 to open faster," the account posted on Monday, Nov 16; IEEE 2030.5 is a communications standard utilities use to manage distributed energy resources. And Wall Connector for Business, Tesla's program for workplace and hospitality chargers, added 141 sites with 910 Wall Connectors in two weeks: 139 sites and 906 units in the United States and two sites with four units in Canada (post).

IV · New Supercharger & Megacharger Sites

Twenty sites, 238 stalls and a Megacharger near Dallas

Two 24-stall stations in Millbrae, California, and at a Buc-ee's in Tennessee led the week; Iceland got two sites, and an eight-stall Megacharger opened in Mesquite, Texas.

Tesla Charging, the company's charging account on X, announced 20 new Supercharger sites from Monday, Nov 16, through Sunday, Nov 22: 15 in the United States with 204 stalls, four in Europe with 30 and one in Japan with four, for 238 stalls by the count of the volunteer-run tracker supercharge.info, which matches every post. All 20 are listed as open with V4 posts. Nine charge at up to 500 kW, six at 325 kW and five at 250 kW.

The two largest have 24 stalls at 500 kW. The station on CA-82 in Millbrae, California, south of San Francisco, was posted at 10:16 p.m. EST Friday (supercharge.info). The one at the Buc-ee's on Elam Road in Murfreesboro, Tennessee, posted on Monday, is listed as open 24 hours (post; supercharge.info).

Six 16-stall sites

Six stations have 16 stalls. Five charge at 500 kW: next to a Burger King on East Main Street in Barstow, California (post); at a second Buc-ee's, in Benton, Arkansas (post); at Centre Ridge Marketplace in Centreville, Virginia (post); at El Dorado Hills Town Center on Vine Street in El Dorado Hills, California, with one trailer-friendly stall (supercharge.info); and at County Square Market on Peabody Road in Vacaville, California (post). The sixth, at Settlers Green Streetside on White Mountain Highway in North Conway, New Hampshire, is listed at 325 kW (post).

Iceland, Hungary, Wales and Japan

Two sites opened on Iceland's Snæfellsnes peninsula: six stalls at Hótel Snaefellsnes in Vegamót (post) and four on Borgarbraut in Stykkishólmur (post). Debrecen, Hungary, got eight stalls at a Tesco (post), and Welshpool, in Wales, 12 at a Costa Coffee at Buttington Cross (post). All four European sites charge at 250 kW with CCS2 plugs. In Japan, four stalls opened at the AEON Mall in Date, Fukushima Prefecture, listed for Teslas only (post; supercharge.info).

Open to other EVs

Three US sites list both NACS and CCS1 connectors at every stall: 12 stalls at each of two Wegmans stores in New York, on Dick Road in Depew and on Lyell Avenue in Rochester, where one stall is trailer-friendly (supercharge.info), and four at a Starbucks in Snohomish, Washington. The Snohomish site belongs to Cathcart Crossing through Supercharger for Business, Tesla's program for stations owned by other businesses (post). The other US sites, in Cloverdale, California; Stafford Township, New Jersey; Spanish Fort, Alabama; and Des Peres, Missouri, have eight stalls each.

Megacharger

"New Tesla Megacharger: Mesquite, TX (8 stalls)," Tesla Charging posted at 8:04 p.m. EST Tuesday, linking the site's Tesla page. Mesquite lies just east of Dallas. Tesla's third-quarter shareholder update says the company "deployed the first pre-assembled Megacharger to support the Tesla Semi ramp" (Exhibit 99.1).

V · SEC Filings

An email to employees: vote by Dec 3

Tesla filed the investor-relations message it sent staff on Friday, ten days before the annual meeting.

Tesla filed additional proxy materials (DEFA14A) at 5:26 p.m. EST Friday containing a communication it sent to its employees that day, according to the filing. The email from Investor Relations, sent at 12:11 p.m., is headed "Vote Your TSLA Shares Now" and "Vote Before December 3 to Shape Tesla's Future." It says Tesla unveiled Master Plan Part IV in 2025 and in 2026 "started turning it into reality: building on our strong foundation, investing in the infrastructure for our next phase of growth and accelerating the deployment of real-world super intelligence," and asks holders to "vote in line with the Board's recommendations on all proposals." Questions go to proxy solicitor Innisfree M&A.

  • DEFA14A · Nov 20, 5:26 p.m. EST — Investor Relations email to employees urging a vote before the Dec 3 annual meeting. Filing
  • Latest Form 4s · Sep 9 — Chief Financial Officer Vaibhav Taneja sold 2,605.75 shares on Sep 8 at $360.134 after 6,539 restricted stock units vested on Sep 5, leaving him 25,972.25 shares. Filing
