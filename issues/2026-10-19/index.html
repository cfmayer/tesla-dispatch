The Morning Picture

A December vote on Musk, Denholm and Straubel

Tesla filed its proxy before dawn Monday: shareholders meet December 3 at Gigafactory Texas to re-elect Elon Musk, chair Robyn Denholm and JB Straubel, cast an advisory vote on 2025 pay and ratify the auditor, according to the DEF 14A. There is no merger proposal, despite a prediction market putting the odds of a Tesla-SpaceX deal before March at 56%, CNBC reported.

Over the weekend, Slovakia cleared FSD (Supervised) and Austin robotaxi rides were extended to midnight. Musk wrote that "Optimus and Cybercab will be the vast majority of Tesla's value." TSLA fell 2.13% to $354.72 on Friday as the Nasdaq dropped, and third-quarter results come Wednesday.

This week · Q3 earnings

Third-quarter results on Wednesday

Wed Oct 21 · results after the close · webcast 5:30 p.m. EDT

Tesla set the date in an 8-K on October 2, when it reported 486,532 deliveries and 13.7 GWh of storage deployed. Analysts expect earnings of $0.45 a share on $28.11 billion in revenue, according to Yahoo Finance (25 and 28 estimates, retrieved Monday). Goldman Sachs says the robotaxi and Optimus outlook will matter more than the quarter itself, Benzinga reported.

I · Annual Meeting

Three proposals, and a new way for small holders to vote

The proxy sets a December 3 meeting in Austin and online; the record date was October 13.

What is on the ballot

  • Directors. Re-elect Class I directors Elon Musk, Robyn Denholm and JB Straubel to three-year terms.
  • Pay. An advisory "say on pay" vote on 2025 executive compensation.
  • Auditor. Ratify PricewaterhouseCoopers for fiscal 2026.

The board recommends a vote for all three. The meeting starts at 4:00 p.m. EST on December 3 (3:00 p.m. local time), online and with limited seating at Gigafactory Texas; votes are due by 11:59 p.m. EST on December 2, according to the voting notice.

Pay and pledged shares

Musk's 2025 CEO Performance Award of about 423.7 million restricted shares is reported at $158.4 billion in the summary compensation table, and none of its 12 tranches has been earned, Reuters reported from the filing. The proxy says the board amended its pledging policy to cap loans backed by the chief executive's pledged stock at the lesser of $3.5 billion or 25% of its value. Musk owns nearly 20% of Tesla, including 207.5 million pledged shares, Reuters said.

Retail voting program

Tesla says it obtained a no-action letter from SEC staff after "months of discussions" to let retail investors set standing voting instructions. Holders can enroll through ProxyVote.com or Say by Robinhood, according to a separate filing and a new investor-relations page.

II · Full Self-Driving (FSD) & Robotaxi

Slovakia says yes; Austin rides run to midnight

Two small steps for the supervised and driverless programs.

Slovakia

Slovakia's transport ministry recognized the Dutch regulator RDW's April approval of FSD (Supervised) on Friday, so drivers can use it on Slovak roads, the ministry said. "It is not a fully autonomous system," Transport Minister Jozef Ráž said, as translated by TeslaNorth, which counts Slovakia as the ninth EU country to clear it. "Tesla supervised self-driving approved in Slovakia," Musk posted.

Austin

Tesla's Austin robotaxi service now runs from 6 a.m. to midnight, an hour later than before, and the Robotaxi app is notifying riders, Not a Tesla App reported on Saturday. Tesla's AI chief Ashok Elluswamy has said FSD v15 will allow round-the-clock service, the site noted.

Quick Hit

Bigger text. "We will add a large font option to the UI," Musk wrote on Sunday (Not a Tesla App).

III · The Roadster

"I fart in your general direction"

Musk spent the weekend promising more from the car and swatting at a critic.

Yann LeCun, the Turing Award winner who was Meta's chief AI scientist, wrote on Saturday that compressed air gives "crappy specific impulse," probably "5 seconds" of useful thrust, and called the flying Roadster a "cute demo, but totally impractical," EVXL reported. Musk replied on Sunday at 10:41 a.m. EDT with the Monty Python line, "I fart in your general direction." Tesla's engineers had told Top Gear that one fill gives about five seconds of thrust, EVXL noted.

What Musk promised

  • "I think we can reach a peak speed of >0.5 Mach or ~400 mph" (post).
  • Roadster "will crush the best Formula 1 track times" with suction and active control surfaces, "both of which are banned by F1" (post).
  • "You will be able to ask the Roadster to perform any of the tricks shown autonomously," plus "a wild looping track" at Tesla's Texas headquarters (post).
  • Future demos may include "flying over the Snake River or maybe Grand Canyon and landing on its wheels" (post).

He also put the car in its place: "Optimus and Cybercab will be the vast majority of Tesla's value," he wrote on Sunday, and "Roadster is super cool, so we're doing it anyway," according to Benzinga.
