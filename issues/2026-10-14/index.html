The Morning Picture

Roadster goes live Thursday night; HW3 cars get a distilled FSD

Tesla has set a start time for the Roadster demonstration: the livestream begins Thursday at 7:55 p.m. Central, 8:55 p.m. EDT, Tesla posted on Tuesday night. Elon Musk added: "Timing is everything." Release notes for software 2026.38.5.1 describe a version of FSD (Supervised) for older Hardware 3 (HW3) cars trained using the newer HW4 software as a guide.

US Model 3 and Model Y buyers can now power their homes from the car in an outage with Powershare Home Backup. In Canada, the cheaper China-built Model 3 is off the order page until March 2027. TSLA slipped 0.74% to $375.23 on about half its usual volume. Tesla has filed nothing new with the SEC.

Tomorrow · Roadster livestream

The new Roadster is shown live Thursday night

Thu Oct 15 · stream starts 8:55 p.m. EDT (7:55 p.m. Central) · live on X

Tesla announced the start time on X on Tuesday night. The invite-only event is outdoors near Waco, Texas, and was moved from October 1 because of high winds, Not a Tesla App reported. What to watch: whether Tesla demonstrates the SpaceX cold-gas thrusters Musk has promoted, the final specifications and price, and a production date. The only official numbers are still from 2017: 0–60 mph in 1.9 seconds, more than 250 mph, 620 miles of range and a $200,000 base price.

I · Full Self-Driving (FSD)

HW4 lessons for HW3 cars, and a closer look at school zones

New release notes promise a better FSD for the oldest supported hardware; NHTSA's robotaxi petition draws national coverage.

Software 2026.38.5.1

The release notes for Tesla software 2026.38.5.1, published by Tessie, say Tesla "distilled the intelligence from HW4 V14 into HW3," letting HW3 learn "using HW4 V14 as a guide," which "unlocks the improvements that have been made to HW4 including Reinforcement Learning (RL)." The notes list fewer false slowdowns, smoother steering, better handling of merges, pedestrians, traffic lights and cut-ins, and new Arrival Options that let the driver choose whether FSD parks in a lot, on the street or in a driveway. Speed Profiles are now available at all times. Musk posted on Tuesday: "Tesla self-driving feels like magic."

School zones

The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) petition over robotaxis allegedly speeding through active school zones reached national outlets. NHTSA says it is evaluating allegations that Tesla's 1,000 or so robotaxis are exceeding posted speed limits in school zones, The Verge reported; Automotive News also covered it. NHTSA has until about January 21, 2027, to decide whether to open a formal investigation.

II · Sales & Products

The cheaper Model 3 leaves Canada's order page until March

Tesla cites the quota on Chinese-built EVs; the government's tracker shows most permits unused.

Model 3 Premium paused

Tesla has stopped taking Canadian orders for the Shanghai-built Model 3 Premium, the trim that brought the sedan down to C$39,490, saying it has used up its import quota for Chinese-made EVs and does not expect to reopen orders until March 2027, Electrek reported. The Canadian government's own tracker shows 33,069 of the 49,000 permits for China-made EVs in the current quota year still available. The only Model 3 left to order in Canada is the Performance AWD.

Quick Hit

Paris. The Mondial de l'Auto's public days run through Sunday, with the Cybercab on Tesla's stand and assisted-driving test drives in Model 3s, according to Tesla Mag.

III · Energy & Charging

Model 3 and Model Y can now back up a house

Powershare Home Backup reaches US Model 3 and Model Y cars ordered from October 1, with a Powerwall 3.

Powershare Home Backup

Tesla has extended Powershare Home Backup to the Model 3 and Model Y, electrive reported. Compatibility is guaranteed only for cars ordered in the US and Puerto Rico from October 1, 2026; some earlier cars may work depending on their hardware. The system needs a Powerwall 3 and a compatible Wall Connector and supplies up to 11.5 kW of continuous power. Owners who have the equipment must contact Tesla support to switch the feature on. Support for Powerwall 2 and Powerwall+ is planned, with no date.

Quick Hit

Panhandle charging. Free Supercharging for Hurricane Isaias relief continues through Sunday at Gulf Breeze, Destin and Pensacola, Florida, Tesla Charging posted.
