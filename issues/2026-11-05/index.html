The Morning Picture

Cybercabs winter-test in Detroit

About two dozen gold Cybercabs are running out of Tesla's Southfield collision-repair facility on 12 Mile Road, with temporary steering wheels and human test drivers, The Detroit News (via the Jefferson City News Tribune) reported Wednesday. The cars have been seen on I-696, M-10, Telegraph Road and Eight Mile Road, putting Tesla's camera-only robotaxi into Michigan's multi-season weather alongside Waymo's driverless Zeekr fleet.

Tesla has not confirmed a Detroit public-service date. Cybercab is open to the public only in Austin so far; Model Y Robotaxis already run unsupervised in Texas and Florida cities. The purpose-built two-seater is due in Dallas later this month, Not a Tesla App reported.

I · Full Self-Driving (FSD)

Detroit roads, and streaks on legacy S/X

Supervised Cybercab testing expands into a snow-belt market; FSD Streak celebrations reach Intel HW3 Model S and Model X.

The Cybercabs operating in Metro Detroit carry temporary steering wheels and test drivers under nondisclosure agreements, The Detroit News reported. The two-seat coupe is designed to have no steering wheel once unsupervised service begins. Waymo began fully autonomous testing in the region in October without human specialists; a Waymo spokesman said there is still no timeline for public deployment. Stephanie Brinley of Mobility Global said expanding autonomous testing across weather and cities matters for the technology, and noted Tesla has not officially said it is testing in Detroit.

Tesla's Robotaxi site says the service has logged more than 4 million miles. Waymo cites 270 million miles across its cities. Cybercab uses cameras only; Waymo combines lidar, radar and cameras. Specs reported for Cybercab include a roughly 48 kWh battery, about 3,000 pounds curb weight and range just shy of 300 miles (Detroit News).

FSD Streaks on Intel HW3 S and X

Software update 2026.38.6.2 adds FSD Streak celebrations to legacy Model S and Model X cars with Intel infotainment and Hardware 3, Not a Tesla App reported. Streaks reward consecutive miles driven without disengaging FSD, with milestone screens at distances such as 250, 500, 1,000 and 5,000 miles. The update also adds the Launch Series badge animation on Model Y L, improves Service Mode language switching and high-voltage isolation detail, and removes Grok's beta label.

Cybercab Europe tour opens in Zurich

Tesla's Cybercab Europe tour opens Thursday in Zurich, according to Tesla's events page. A South Korean consumer group planned to file its FSD complaint with the Korea Fair Trade Commission on Thursday, Reuters reported earlier this week.

II · Engineering & Vehicles

Berlin Engineering Center opens

Four sites become one campus in Köpenick, 30 minutes from Giga Berlin; Tesla asks Cybertruck owners for videos.

"Berlin Tesla Engineering Center opens today. From permit to operational in under a year, it brings 4 engineering sites onto 1 campus w/ 30 different labs in Köpenick, 30 minutes from Giga Berlin. With our 3rd hub of this kind after Fremont & Shanghai, we're combining materials, powertrain, vehicle engineering & validation under one roof – enabling a short feedback loop between development & ramp," @teslaeurope posted at 2:41 p.m. EST Wednesday.

"Berlin is awesome and I am excited to officially open our Berlin Engineering Center! It was great to bring together the Tesla team with the local government officials as the start of this great partnership," Tesla executive Wes Morrill wrote at 3:27 a.m. EST Thursday.

"Show us how Cybertruck has changed your life"

Tesla quoted a Cybertruck account at 9:25 p.m. EST Wednesday that asked owners to "Show us how Cybertruck has changed your life" in video replies, ideally 60 seconds or less.

III · Energy & Charging

36 new stalls in California, plus Asia opens

Santa Ana and Castaic open in Southern California; Petaling Jaya and Tampines add four stalls each.

Tesla opened a 16-stall Supercharger on North Grand Avenue in Santa Ana, California, @TeslaCharging posted at 5:15 p.m. EST Wednesday, and a 12-stall site at 31783 Castaic Road in Castaic at 12:56 p.m. EST (post). Overnight, four-stall sites opened at IPC in Petaling Jaya, Malaysia, and at Tampines Mall in Singapore (Petaling Jaya; Tampines).
