The Morning Picture

Results tomorrow, and a new FSD build today

Tesla reports its third quarter after Wednesday's close. The 23 analysts in the company's own compiled consensus, posted Monday, expect $28.34 billion in revenue, 45 cents a share in adjusted earnings and negative free cash flow of $4.51 billion as capital spending runs near $7.8 billion. On Tuesday the company began rolling out software update 2026.38.5.10 with FSD (Supervised) v14.3.13 for AI4 cars and v14.3 Lite for HW3, Not a Tesla App lists.

In a letter filed Monday evening, the board asked shareholders to re-elect Elon Musk, Robyn Denholm and JB Straubel and said FSD now has nearly 1.5 million paid customers. TSLA rose 3.76% to $368.06 on Monday as AI stocks rebounded and Tesla gave the consumer sector its second-biggest lift, Reuters reported.

Tomorrow · Q3 earnings

Third-quarter results on Wednesday

Wed Oct 21 · results after the close · live Q&A webcast 5:30 p.m. EDT

The date comes from Tesla's October 2 8-K; the shareholder update and webcast will be on ir.tesla.com. The company-compiled consensus (23 firms, Oct 19) has automotive revenue at $20.27 billion, energy at $3.72 billion and services at $4.35 billion; gross margin at 17.9%; operating income at $721 million; and GAAP earnings of 23 cents a share. Yahoo Finance shows 45 cents on $28.11 billion (25 and 28 estimates), while Reuters cites 43 cents on $27.88 billion. A year ago Tesla earned 50 cents a share on $28.10 billion. Investors are focused on robotaxis, Optimus and spending: Tesla has said capital spending will top $25 billion this year, Reuters noted.

I · Full Self-Driving (FSD)

v14.3.13 starts rolling out

A rebuilt compiler, a new collision-evasion feature and one model for FSD, Summon and Robotaxi.

What is in the build

FSD (Supervised) v14.3.13 upgrades the reinforcement-learning stage of training and the vision encoder, and "rewrote the AI compiler and runtime from the ground up with MLIR, resulting in 20% faster reaction time," according to the release notes published by Teslascope. It "unified the model between Actually Smart Summon, FSD, and Robotaxi," raises Actually Smart Summon's top speed to 8 mph, improves handling of emergency vehicles, school buses, small animals and compound traffic lights, and shows the distance driven without an intervention. Tesla lists pothole avoidance as an upcoming improvement.

The same update adds Automatic Collision Evasion for HW4 cars, which "activates Full Self-Driving (Supervised) to try to keep your vehicle safe and then continue driving" when it detects an inattentive driver, according to Not a Tesla App. HW3 cars get v14.3 Lite, which "distilled the intelligence from HW4 V14 into HW3" and adds parking, unparking, reversing and arrival options. The rollout began Tuesday and was in its earliest stage, with TeslaFi counting a single car, the site showed.

Quick Hit

Also in the update. 2026.38.5.10 lets owners set a trip's arrival charge from the phone app, adds a Halloween mode with a ghost costume for the car on screen, drops the beta label from Grok, and gives HW3 cars a "Sloth" speed profile that drives slower than Chill, Not a Tesla App lists.

The board's tally

Tesla's shareholder letter says it grew FSD (Supervised) "usage exponentially, reaching nearly 1.5 million paid customers and more than 15 billion cumulative miles driven to date," expanded Robotaxi to seven U.S. cities and deployed Cybercabs "without steering wheels or pedals in our Robotaxi fleet," according to the filing. Tesla's Robotaxi account said on October 10 that it had "scaled to 300+ Cybercabs serving Austin," and Ashok Elluswamy, Tesla's head of AI software, added "Dallas soon," Teslarati reported; sworn answers to a National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) audit query on the Cybercab's safety certification are due October 30.

II · Sales & Demand

Some Model Y L buyers wait until 2027

Early U.S. orders for the six-seater are slipping by months while others are delivered.

New U.S. orders for the $61,990 Model Y L Launch Series now show delivery from January to March 2027, up from January to February, and some earlier buyers see February to April, Not a Tesla App reported on Monday. One reader told the site a mid-October delivery had been "pushed out 3 to 4 months," while an owner in South Florida took delivery last week. Tesla has not commented. The six-seat crossover went on sale in the U.S. in early July with first deliveries planned for September.

Quick Hit

Quarter so far. Tesla delivered 486,532 vehicles in the third quarter, beating estimates, and is on track to grow deliveries this year for the first time since 2023, Reuters noted; the board's letter says Tesla has delivered more than 10 million vehicles to date.

III · Energy & Charging

Storm Watch, site maps and four new Superchargers

The energy business is expected to bring in $3.72 billion in the quarter.

"Electricity goes out, I know my family's safe, and that means a lot to me," a Powerwall owner in Hawaii says in a video Tesla Energy posted on Monday, explaining how Storm Watch charges the battery ahead of a storm. The board's letter says Tesla has deployed more than 130 gigawatt-hours of storage to date, with record deployments from Megafactory Shanghai, and has begun producing Megapack 3 in Texas.

Update 2026.38.5.10 brings Supercharger site maps with live stall occupancy at pilot locations and "Emergency Drive Away," which lets a driver shift into Drive with the cable still connected, at the risk of damage, according to Not a Tesla App. Tesla Charging announced new eight-stall sites in Cherokee, North Carolina, a voting winner; Bolu, Türkiye (post); and two in Goyang, South Korea (post, post).

IV · The Annual Meeting

"Your vote is critical"

Tesla opened its campaign for the December 3 meeting and a drawing for in-person seats.

"This year, we're asking you to vote 'FOR' all three proposals on the ballot, including the re-election of Elon Musk, Robyn Denholm and JB Straubel to Tesla's Board," Tesla posted at 10:31 a.m. EDT Monday. "Your vote is critical to helping us maintain that momentum." The board's letter calls Musk "the architect of our transformation" and says "continuity of leadership matters," according to the filing, which also lists Innisfree M&A as proxy solicitor.

What the letter points to

The board frames 2026 as the year it began "executing" Part IV of the Master Plan, "designed to create a world of amazing abundance for all." Beyond FSD and Robotaxi, it cites a Semi factory opening in Nevada, a high-volume Optimus production line in Fremont, and the Terafab initiative "to secure supply of critical components at scale," and says Tesla scaled core vehicle production "10x in just six years," according to the filing.

Seats at Gigafactory Texas will go by random drawing among holders as of October 13; entries close at 12:59 a.m. EST on Saturday, November 7, and Tesla expects to notify those selected the week of November 16, VoteTesla.com says. Selections are non-transferable, and any holder as of October 13 can join online with a 16-digit control number.
