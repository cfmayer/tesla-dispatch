The Morning Picture

Cybercabs now outnumber Model Ys in the Texas fleet

Tesla's purpose-built Cybercab has become the majority vehicle in the company's authorized Robotaxi fleet in Texas for the first time, Yahoo Finance reported on Tuesday, citing Texas Department of Motor Vehicles data: of 1,051 authorized vehicles, 544 are Cybercabs and 507 are Model Ys. As recently as early October the registry showed roughly 319 Cybercabs against 420 Model Ys. The data show which vehicles are authorized for Robotaxi service, not whether they are on the road carrying riders.

Registrations kept coming after the crossover. "Tesla just registered another 29 Cybercabs for its Robotaxi fleet, bringing the total number of Cybercabs registered to date to 573, according to the Texas DMV," Sawyer Merritt posted at 10:34 p.m. EDT Tuesday, adding that Tesla had registered 222 in the last week. The Cybercab runs only in Austin for now, and Tesla said on last week's earnings call that it wants to add Cybercabs in Dallas next month. "We want to swarm Austin with Cybercabs everywhere. There's no fundamental reason that we are limited to Austin," AI vice president Ashok Elluswamy said on the call (Yahoo Finance). "Right now, we're building at the rate that the Robotaxi service needs the vehicles," said Lars Moravy, vice president of vehicle engineering.

I · Full Self-Driving (FSD)

Seoul police book 80 owners over FSD devices

Owners of China-built Teslas used unauthorized devices to switch on FSD (Supervised), which Korea allows only on U.S.-built cars.

Seoul police have booked 80 people for activating FSD (Supervised) with unauthorized devices on China-built cars, and seized 21 of the devices, Korea JoongAng Daily reported on Tuesday. They face charges under the Motor Vehicle Management Act, which carries up to two years in prison or a fine of up to 20 million won ($15,000). The transport ministry asked police to investigate in April, according to Yonhap.

The suspects used activation devices bought in Europe, China and elsewhere, or built their own; one, who works in the IT industry, made a device from information found on GitHub, and owners traded tips online on how to keep the activation from leaving records in their cars, police said (JoongAng Daily). "Safety risks were confirmed, including the possibility that FSD may fail to properly recognize driving conditions or operate differently from the driver's intentions," a police official said.

In Korea, FSD is legal only on U.S.-built Teslas such as the Model S, Model X and Cybertruck, which are exempt from Korean safety certification under the Korea-U.S. free trade agreement; the China-made Model 3 and Model Y have not passed Korea's standards. Of the 229,311 Teslas on Korean roads as of August, 47,458, or 20.7%, can legally use the software, according to Carisyou Data Lab (Seoul Economic Daily).

FSD rentals open on Turo

From Wednesday, U.S. guests on Turo can search for and book Teslas with FSD (Supervised) activated, with the status verified through Tesla's API, and eligible hosts get a free one-month trial, the car-sharing company said in its announcement. Renters see cautionary language and an optional Tesla tutorial, and listings can carry a "Self-Driving" label; the Model 3 and Model Y are Turo's third- and fourth-most-popular vehicles this year, TechCrunch reported.

Florida beaches "Coming soon"

"They need to have the Tampa and Miami ones include the beaches!" Florida Governor Ron DeSantis wrote at 10:43 p.m. EDT Monday, quoting a post on Houston's larger map. Elon Musk replied at 10:35 a.m. Tuesday: "Coming soon." Robotaxi's Tampa zone covers 24 square miles and Miami's 31, Sawyer Merritt posted on Monday.

A world champion rides a Cybercab

Damon Hill, the 1996 Formula 1 world champion, took a solo Cybercab ride in Austin. "F1 World Champion tries Cybercab," the Robotaxi account posted at 8:51 p.m. EDT Tuesday, sharing the video. The car greets Hill by name on its screen, and during the ride he asks Grok what happened at the U.S. Grand Prix and has it play race highlights, TeslaNorth reported. "Grok can search for & pull up videos for you, all via voice," the account added.

Cybercab pack leaves room for LFP

The Cybercab packages its 4680 cells in four separate modules, so a faulty module can be repaired or replaced without swapping the whole pack, and Tesla left extra space inside the pack in case it later adopts lower-density lithium iron phosphate cells, Digital Today reported on Wednesday, citing comments by Tesla engineers on the Munro Live channel and other coverage. Whether and when Tesla will move from the current NMC chemistry has not been decided. Engineers said the Cybercab is designed for a 500,000-mile life, and its chief engineer said replacing a full pack before then would be costly (Digital Today).

Waymo widens five maps. Over the next week Waymo is adding nearly 250 square miles across Los Angeles, Nashville, Orlando, San Diego and the San Francisco Bay Area, including Torrance, La Jolla and nearly double the coverage in eastern Orlando, the company said on Tuesday.

II · Sales & Demand

Tesla starts hiring in Vietnam

Five openings cover sales, service, charging, HR and purchasing, after a $3 million subsidiary was registered in September.

Tesla is recruiting for five positions in Vietnam, covering sales, service, charging, human resources and purchasing, TNGlobal reported on Wednesday, citing a post on X by Ricky Ip, a regional recruiting manager at Tesla: "Tesla is officially coming to Vietnam – and we're hiring." Tesla Motors Vietnam LLC was registered in September with charter capital of 77.667 billion dong ($3 million) and headquarters in Ho Chi Minh City; its registered activities include wholesale and retail sales of vehicles and parts, import, export and distribution.

None of the postings describes factory, assembly or manufacturing work, and they do not show launch timing, showroom or charging locations or which models would be sold, TNGlobal said. Vietnam's car market is led by domestic EV maker VinFast, which sold 20,161 of the 48,484 cars sold in the country in August, or 42% (TNGlobal).

III · Energy & Charging

Every new Tesla can run tools with Powershare

Vehicle-to-load comes to new cars in the U.S. and Mexico, and five Supercharger sites opened.

"Every new Tesla can now power your tools or gadgets via Powershare," Tesla North America posted at 4:07 p.m. EDT Tuesday, adding in a reply: "Available in US & Mexico with Mobile Connector and Outlet Adapter." Canada is not included, Tesla News Wire noted.

"With Powershare, your new Tesla can function like a portable battery," Tesla Energy posted at 7:28 p.m. "In the US, it can also power your home with Powerwall 3."

Five new Supercharger sites

Tesla Charging announced five sites with 56 stalls on Tuesday: Wikieup, Arizona, 16 stalls (2:05 p.m. EDT); Simi Valley, California, 16 (4:05 p.m.); Market Street Northeast in Washington, D.C., eight (6:43 p.m.); Bealeton, Virginia, eight (9:14 p.m.); and Laurel, Maryland, eight (9:37 p.m.).

An Emergency Drive Away by mistake

A video posted on Reddit shows a driver at an Austin Supercharger at about 6 a.m. on Sunday, during the F1 weekend, using Tesla's Emergency Drive Away feature, which releases the car from a Supercharger cable so an owner can flee danger; the poster said the driver "only seemed to half-read the prompts," Teslarati reported. The feature still damages the car and the charger, Tesla has said repairs can cost up to $25,000, and a driver who uses it outside an emergency pays for them, according to Teslarati.

Quick Hit

"Like my old Roadster." Tesla posted "What goes up must come down" at 12:49 p.m. EDT Tuesday. Musk replied: "Unless you reach escape velocity, like my old Roadster."
