The Morning Picture

German registrations nearly quadruple

Tesla registered 2,932 new cars in Germany in October, up 290.9% from a year earlier, the KBA motor transport authority said on Wednesday, Reuters reported. From January to October Tesla registered 47,742 cars in Germany, up 206.1% from the same period of 2025.

Battery-electric cars made up 37.0% of all new registrations in Germany in October. Tesla's share of the overall market was 1.2%, against 2.0% for China's BYD, according to the KBA figures cited by Reuters.

I · Full Self-Driving (FSD)

A South Korean complaint over FSD sales

A consumer group says Tesla sold FSD to tens of thousands of buyers whose China-made cars cannot legally use it; the Dutch regulator approved a renamed, slower system.

Tesla misled tens of thousands of South Korean customers by marketing driver-assistance software that the company knows cannot be legally used in most of its vehicles in the country, according to a complaint the advocacy group Citizens United for Consumer Sovereignty plans to file with the Korea Fair Trade Commission (KFTC) on Thursday, Reuters reported. Tesla has often collected lump-sum payments of 9.043 million won ($6,726) for FSD (Supervised) in South Korea, before shifting to a monthly subscription of 150,000 won ($112) in August.

FSD can be used on about 20% of Teslas registered in South Korea, the U.S.-built cars, which meet U.S. safety standards the country recognizes under a free trade agreement, according to Tesla Korea country manager Seo Young-deuk. Tesla has sold more than 170,000 Shanghai-built cars there since 2023, and 99% of new Teslas sold in South Korea last year could not legally use FSD. Tesla applied for certification two weeks after an Oct. 8 hearing. The KFTC opened a probe of Tesla earlier this year. Tesla did not respond to requests for comment, Reuters said.

"Tesla generated substantial revenue by selling an expensive driver-assistance system to mass-market buyers who cannot use it for even a single day," Choi Young-suk said, according to Reuters. The group is asking the KFTC for a cease-and-desist order and fines. Tesla passed BMW this year as the top-selling import brand in South Korea.

Assisted Driving, capped at +10%

The Dutch vehicle authority RDW approved changes to Tesla's system on Tuesday: FSD Supervised is now called Tesla Assisted Driving in Europe, and the maximum speed margin drops from +50% to +10% and resets to 0% at each start. The update applies to cars with HW4 or newer and arrives over the air. A Europe-wide vote has been pushed to December at the earliest, Electrek reported.

"The best car I have ever owned"

"FSD is revolutionary – a step change in what it means to drive. I don't think any other car at a similar price comes close. It's the best car I have ever owned," Tesla posted at 12:44 p.m. EST Tuesday, quoting an owner's post titled "Thoughts on Owning a Self-Driving Tesla."

II · Politics & Markets

Shares slide after Texas flips

Democrats won the Texas governor's race and both chambers of Congress, and the governor-elect wants a pause on new data centers.

Tesla shares fell 3.6% in premarket trading on Wednesday after Democrat Gina Hinojosa defeated Gov. Greg Abbott in Texas and Democrats won both chambers of Congress, Reuters reported. A 7:36 a.m. EST report put the decline at 5.6%, Investing.com reported, as investors weighed more scrutiny of Musk's companies and complications for a possible combination with SpaceX.

Hinojosa campaigned on a moratorium on new data-center construction until the legislature sets additional requirements, including union labor, more community input, protections for local water and electricity, and rules that operators pay their own infrastructure costs, Barclays analysts noted, according to Investing.com. Wells Fargo puts 27% of planned U.S. data-center gigawatts in Texas, CNBC reported.

The merger question

Tesla shares have climbed 34% since their late-July lows, helped partly by speculation about a combination with SpaceX. That deal remains exploratory, and Bloomberg has reported an effort to announce one in the first half of next year, Investing.com said; a more hostile political environment could make regulatory approval harder. Musk reportedly gave nearly $450 million to Republican causes.

III · Energy & Charging

Trade-zone status, but China tariffs stay

The Brookshire Megapack plant can defer duties, but Chinese LFP cells still pay the Section 301 rate.

The foreign-trade zone approval for Tesla's Megapack factory in Brookshire, Texas, does not exempt the Chinese lithium iron phosphate (LFP) cells inside, Electrek reported. Tesla's application for Subzone 84AM says parts subject to Section 122, 232 or 301 tariffs must enter the zone in "privileged foreign status," which locks in their own tariff treatment. A Chinese cell in a Megapack sold in the U.S. still pays the Section 301 tariff on Chinese non-EV lithium-ion batteries, which rose from 7.5% to 25% on Jan. 1, 2026; Tesla pays it when the finished Megapack leaves the zone.

What Tesla gets: duties deferred until shipment, no U.S. duty on Megapacks exported straight from the zone, the lower finished-product rate on parts not caught by special tariffs, and no duty on scrap. Production was "authorized on a limited basis" for 14 months, according to the Federal Register notice, which puts expiry around the end of 2027, roughly when LG Energy Solution is due to start supplying LFP cells from Michigan under a $4.3 billion Megapack 3 contract, Electrek said. Brookshire is designed for 50 GWh a year, on top of the 40 GWh Lathrop plant in California.

"And electrical equipment, cooling, networking and batteries"

Musk added that list at 11:07 a.m. EST Tuesday to a post summarizing his Dwarkesh Patel podcast interview, which said "the bottleneck for the next year is not chips, it is raw power."

Four stalls in Oberkrämer

Tesla opened a four-stall Supercharger in Oberkrämer, Germany, @TeslaCharging posted at 1:35 p.m. EST Tuesday.

IV · Software & Vehicles

Grok comes out of beta in the car

Software update 2026.38.6 went to wide release, and Tesla showed the new Roadster from the driver's seat.

Tesla software update 2026.38.6, now in wide release, removes the "Beta" label from the Grok interface on vehicle screens and adds Latvian to the on-screen languages, Tesla Oracle reported.

The Roadster from the driver's seat

Tesla posted a point-of-view clip at 11:47 a.m. EST Tuesday from the next-generation Roadster's Oct. 15 unveiling at SpaceX's McGregor, Texas, test site. The uncrewed prototype fires cold-gas thrusters and rolls fully inverted over a Model 3, Model Y, Cybercab, Cybertruck and Semi; all runs were autonomous, Teslarati reported. Production is targeted for 2028, the site said.
