The Morning Picture

FSD becomes "Tesla Assisted Driving" in Europe, as regulators look at school-zone speeding

Tesla has renamed Full Self-Driving (FSD) (Supervised) "Tesla Assisted Driving" on its European websites, meeting a condition set by Germany's transport minister, who now says he will push for approval across the European Union (EU). In the US, the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) has opened a file on a petition alleging that Tesla robotaxis sped through active school zones, and estimates the fleet at about 1,000 vehicles. Tesla's Robotaxi account said service now runs from 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. and that Grok Bot is available in the Cybercab.

A Cybercab from the Austin fleet was unveiled at the Paris Motor Show's press day on Monday; the public gets in from today. A Taiwanese power company disclosed an order for 260 Megapacks, and Tesla is offering free Supercharging in three Florida Panhandle cities hit by Hurricane Isaias. TSLA fell 1.22% to $378.03 on light volume. Tesla has filed nothing new with the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC). The Roadster demonstration is Thursday.

I · Full Self-Driving (FSD)

A new name for Europe, and a federal file on school zones

Germany's minister got the rename he asked for and now backs EU-wide approval. NHTSA has 120 days to decide whether a robotaxi speeding petition becomes a defect investigation.

"Tesla Assisted Driving"

Tesla agreed to rename the system after German Transport Minister Steffen Bilger called "FSD" misleading, and the new name has replaced the old label on Tesla's websites in Germany and many other European countries, though not worldwide, electrive reported. "It is not a system that takes over the full driving task," the ministry said; the driver "must remain attentive at all times." Bilger said he reached "a constructive agreement" with Tesla in September:

"On this basis, I will now advocate for the system to be approved across Europe as soon as possible."
Steffen Bilger · German Federal Minister of Transport, via electrive

Germany was among the countries whose concerns delayed a vote in the EU's Technical Committee on Motor Vehicles (TCMV), now expected in December. The rename changes nothing about how the system works. In France it appears as "Tesla Conduite Assistée," Tesla Mag reported; in the US it is still sold as FSD (Supervised). Missy Cummings, an engineering professor at George Mason University, said the rename "ignores both past and ongoing NHTSA investigations," The Drive reported.

NHTSA: the school-zone petition

NHTSA's Office of Defects Investigation opened defect petition DP26010 on Friday, October 9, over claims that model-year 2026 robotaxis running Tesla's Automated Driving System sped through active school zones, Electrek reported. The evidence is three social media videos and four incidents in NHTSA's tally, with no crashes or injuries. The agency estimates about 1,000 affected Model Y and Cybercab vehicles, the first federal figure for the fleet's size, though Electrek noted it may reflect vehicles registered in Texas rather than in service. NHTSA has until about January 21, 2027, to grant or deny the petition; a grant opens a formal investigation, which can end in a recall. It is the third federal action on the driverless fleet in about five weeks, after an audit query and a Special Order on the Cybercab's certification, whose answers are due October 30.

Robotaxi: longer hours, Grok Bot aboard

Tesla's Robotaxi account listed service changes on Monday at 4:27 p.m. EDT: operating hours are now 6 a.m. to 11 p.m., wait times are shorter "thanks to increased supply & orchestration algorithms," the trunk opens automatically at the end of a ride if it was used, and Grok Bot is now available in the Cybercab. Elon Musk quoted it with "Robotaxi improvements."

II · Sales & Products

The Cybercab's European debut, on a plinth

Paris gets a car from the Austin fleet to look at, test drives in Model 3s, and no European robotaxi plan.

Paris Motor Show

Tesla staff pulled the cover off a gold production Cybercab on stand C25 in Hall 7.1 on Monday morning, the press day of the Mondial de l'Auto, with an Optimus robot in a glass case beside it, CyberCab.News reported. Visitors can walk around it but not ride in it. Tesla is offering free test drives of its assisted-driving system in Model 3s from today through Sunday; the show is open from 9:30 a.m. to 8 p.m. Paris time (3:30 a.m. to 2 p.m. EDT), according to Tesla Mag. Only Tesla and XPeng were invited to offer assisted-driving test drives at the show, XPeng's chief executive said. A European Cybercab service is not planned at this stage, French outlet Turbo reported, according to CyberCab.News.

Quick Hit

Quebec court. Tesla Canada must pay C$161,700 to an insurer for a 2020 Model X that burned in Montreal. An expert traced the fire to a dashboard electrical failure, and the judge rejected Tesla's argument that car fires "generally" stem from misuse or vandalism, Electrek reported.

III · Energy & Charging

About 1 GWh of Megapacks for Taiwan, and free charging after Isaias

A stock-exchange filing in Taipei sized a new storage order; the Semi's charging network added its third site in three days.

Apollo Power orders 260 Megapacks

Taiwan's Apollo Power signed a contract with Tesla Motors Taiwan on October 8 to buy 260 Megapack 2XL units, roughly 1 gigawatt-hour (GWh) of storage, according to a filing with the Taiwan Stock Exchange on October 11, TeslaNorth reported. The units will be delivered in batches for behind-the-meter business storage and grid-resilience projects. The price was not disclosed under a confidentiality agreement.

Hurricane relief

Tesla is offering free Supercharging from October 13 through 18 at its Gulf Breeze, Destin and Pensacola, Florida, stations for Hurricane Isaias relief, Tesla Charging posted on Monday. Isaias came ashore near Destin at about 9:30 p.m. EDT on October 9 as a Category 2 hurricane with 105 mph winds, the National Hurricane Center said. Florida Governor Ron DeSantis thanked Tesla, and Musk replied, "Of course."

Semi Megachargers

A four-stall Semi Megacharger opened at the Flying J in Tulare, California, Tesla's third new site in three days after Ellabell and Conley, Georgia, Drive Tesla reported. Eleven more locations with Pilot are under construction. Tesla Semi's Dan Priestley put California energy costs at more than $1.00 a mile for diesel against about $0.60 a mile for a Semi on the Megacharger network.

IV · Chips, Robots & Roadster

Roadster Thursday: the date is set, the details are not

Tesla's outdoor Roadster demonstration near Waco, Texas, is set for Thursday, October 15, after at least five earlier dates, Tesla News Wire reported. Tesla has not posted a start time or livestream. All Things Elon reported that Musk confirmed a 6 p.m. Pacific start (9 p.m. EDT). The only official specifications are still those from 2017: 0–60 mph in 1.9 seconds, more than 250 mph, up to 620 miles of range and a $200,000 base price. Production is guided for "2027 or 2028" at Giga Texas.
