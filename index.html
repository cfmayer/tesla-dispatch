The Morning Picture

Seoul court rejects owners' FSD refund suit

A Seoul court on Thursday dismissed all claims by Tesla owners who bought the Full Self-Driving option between 2019 and 2022 and sued for their money back, ruling that the purchase was not a contractual promise of fully driverless, Level 5 autonomy. The 30th civil division of the Seoul Central District Court, presided over by Judge Kim Seok-beom, ruled against 89 owners who had each paid about 9 million won for the option and filed suit in December 2024, the Seoul Economic Daily reported; CNA put the number of plaintiffs at 88.

The court treated the sale as a contract with an uncertain delivery term, found that fitting cars with Hardware 3 rather than Hardware 4 was not non-performance, and said delays to further features stemmed from regulatory approval rather than a breach, according to the Seoul Economic Daily and SBS. CNA noted that a recent update brought traffic-light and stop-sign recognition and city-street driving to Hardware 3 cars in Korea. The decision is at the trial-court level (Star News).

I · Full Self-Driving (FSD)

Italy's Salvini backs swift EU approval

The transport minister spoke with Elon Musk; Robotaxi starts giving first rides free.

"Very positive and constructive conversation with @elonmusk today," Matteo Salvini, Italy's deputy prime minister and minister of infrastructure and transport, posted at 4:21 a.m. EDT Thursday. "Italy is looking with great interest at the opportunities offered by assisted driving technologies, with a focus on innovation and road safety, and supports a swift EU approval of Tesla Assisted Driving," the name FSD (Supervised) carries in Europe (LaPresse; Teslarati).

Latvia on Monday became the tenth EU country to accept the Dutch provisional type approval (Teslarati). An EU-wide decision needs a qualified majority in the Technical Committee on Motor Vehicles, at least 15 of 27 member states representing 65% of the bloc's population, and a vote is expected in December at the earliest, Electrek reported. Germany, France and Italy remain under review, Teslarati said.

A free first Robotaxi ride

"Robotaxi now gives people their first ride for free," Ethan McKanna posted at 10:09 p.m. EDT Wednesday. The offer appears in the Robotaxi app for new riders in Dallas and Houston but not in Austin, and Tesla had not confirmed it, TeslaNorth reported. Earlier Wednesday the Robotaxi account said at 4:34 p.m. that the app's Live Activities "now have a new look."

"Robotaxi Houston"

Elon Musk posted "Robotaxi Houston" at 1:49 p.m. EDT Wednesday, quoting a Houston Police Officers' Union post that urged people not to drink and drive and noted that Tesla is expanding its Robotaxi service area across the city. At 10:17 a.m. he wrote "At Tesla, we even care about the chipmunks," quoting a clip of a 98-year-old Model Y owner who said his car stopped for a chipmunk crossing the road.

The fifth batch of v14.3.13

New builds of FSD (Supervised) v14.3.13 and v14.3 Lite are rolling out to a fifth batch of owners with software 2026.38.5.11, Tesla Newswire posted at 2:46 p.m. EDT Wednesday. The release brings parking, unparking and reversing to Hardware 3 cars, along with Arrival Options and Speed Profiles, according to Not a Tesla App; TeslaFi showed about 3,600 cars, or 29% of those it tracks, on the build (FSD Update).

Quick Hit

0.18% unsupervised. FSD (Supervised) is running at 316.8 million miles a week against 557,686 unsupervised miles, Nic Cruz Patane posted at 8:00 p.m. EDT Wednesday.

GM calls vision-only a "religious stance"

General Motors will keep radar in its autonomous systems, GM's Sterling Anderson told The Verge, calling a vision-only approach a "weird religious stance that ignores physics." "If you scale back to vision-only, physics suggests you'll be in trouble in certain situations. Radar is so cheap," he said, as Sawyer Merritt quoted.

II · Sales & Demand

Ford's EV unit loses $1.06 billion

Model e's third-quarter loss narrowed from a year earlier as revenue fell with the end of the F-150 Lightning.

Ford's Model e electric-vehicle unit posted a third-quarter loss before interest and taxes of $1.06 billion on revenue of $1.2 billion, about 25% better than a year earlier, the company said in its results release on Wednesday. Ford still expects Model e to lose about $4 billion in 2026 (CNBC).

Model e revenue fell 33% from a year earlier, "driven by discontinuation of F-150 Lightning and right-sizing of Mustang Mach-E," Ford said, as Sawyer Merritt quoted at 4:19 p.m. EDT, putting the unit's margin at −86.5%.

The Roadster visits Hawthorne

Tesla brought the next-generation Roadster to SpaceX's headquarters in Hawthorne, California, on Wednesday, Sawyer Merritt posted at 4:26 p.m. EDT, noting the site is next to the Tesla design studio where the car was designed. The car was displayed beside the retired Falcon 9 booster B1019 outside the building, Teslarati reported.

III · Energy & Charging

Voters pick 36 new Supercharger sites

Winners stretch from Miyazu, Japan, to Kulu, Turkey, and three new sites added 28 stalls.

"New winners announced on our map and a new Supercharger voting round is now open," Tesla Charging posted at 6:35 p.m. EDT Wednesday. The 36 winning locations, listed in a reply, include Miyazu, Japan; Genting Sempah, Malaysia; Tábor, Czech Republic; Agrigento, Italy; Kulu, Konya, Turkey; Lossiemouth and Puddletown in the United Kingdom; Corregidora, Mexico; Sainte-Anne-des-Monts, Quebec; and Clarkdale, Arizona. Votes are cast on Tesla's Supercharger voting page.

Three new sites, 28 stalls

Tesla Charging announced Hudson, Wisconsin, 12 stalls (2:03 p.m. EDT Wednesday); Norwood, Ontario, eight (8:36 p.m.); and Hannover Südstadt-Bult, Germany, eight (2:22 a.m. Thursday).

Quick Hit

Charge cheap, spend later. "Powerwall charges up when power is cheap, so you can use it when rates are the highest," Tesla Energy posted at 7:42 p.m. EDT Wednesday.

IV · Semi & Software

A Tesla Semi burns on I-880

The fire closed southbound lanes in Hayward for about ten hours; no crash was involved.

A Tesla Semi caught fire on southbound Interstate 880 in Hayward, California, at about 8:35 p.m. EDT Tuesday without a collision, closing the southbound lanes for about ten hours until roughly 6:30 a.m. EDT Wednesday, Electrek reported. It is the second known Semi fire, after one on Interstate 80 in August 2024, according to Teslarati.

Trick or Treat mode

"Trick or treaters beware," Tesla posted at 8:45 p.m. EDT Wednesday. "Enable Trick or Treat and your Tesla will play spooky sounds & lights when visitors approach," the company added, under Toybox, then Halloween Mode.

Quick Hit

Musk at Baron. Musk will join Ron Baron for a virtual fireside chat at the 33rd Annual Baron Investment Conference on Friday, Nov. 6, starting at 1:10 p.m. EST (Baron Capital; TeslaNorth).
