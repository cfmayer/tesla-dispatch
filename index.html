The Morning Picture

China-made sales rise for a 12th month

Tesla sold 67,829 cars made at its Shanghai plant in October, including exports to Europe, Asia Pacific and Canada, up 10.3% from a year earlier, according to China Passenger Car Association data reported by Reuters on Tuesday. That was faster than September's 5% annual gain and the 12th straight month of growth, though sales fell 28.9% from September's 95,366, the final month of the third quarter. October's total was the lowest since February, CnEVPost noted.

In Europe, Tesla registered 6,742 cars in October across seven markets that have reported so far, up 69% from 3,985 a year earlier, according to national data compiled by EV. October 2025 was one of Tesla's weakest months in the region, so the comparison is easy, and October is usually the slowest month of a quarter because Tesla concentrates deliveries toward quarter-end.

I · Full Self-Driving (FSD)

Fort Worth rides start with no safety monitor

Tesla's seventh Robotaxi city opened Monday with unsupervised rides across about 143 square miles, and the FSD fleet passed 17 billion miles.

Tesla's Robotaxi service began in Fort Worth, Texas, at 6 a.m. Central (7 a.m. EST) on Monday, with unsupervised rides and no safety monitor on board, Not a Tesla App reported. The site put the service area at about 143 square miles, stretching from Saginaw in the north to White Settlement in the west and Benbrook in the south, which would make it Tesla's third-largest unsupervised area after Austin and Houston. Fort Worth was not one of the four cities Tesla had listed as next in line: Las Vegas, Phoenix, Jacksonville and San Antonio. The site also reported that Cybercab is due in Dallas later this month.

A Fort Worth Report reporter rode from the Near Southside District to a downtown restaurant in about 15 minutes, "navigating substantial midday traffic," the Fort Worth Report wrote. "The app initially canceled the ride before charging $8.98 to the reporter's credit card three times for the one trip." Fort Worth joins Austin, Dallas and Houston in Texas and Miami, Orlando and Tampa in Florida, the paper said.

Tesla recently extended Robotaxi hours in Austin to 2 a.m. and began offering free first rides to new riders, a promotion spotted so far in Houston and Dallas, Not a Tesla App said. Counting the 1,098-square-mile Bay Area zone, where rides still carry a safety driver, Fort Worth is Tesla's fourth-largest service area.

17 billion miles

Cars using FSD (Supervised) have driven more than 17 billion miles, about 6.76 billion of them on city streets, and the software is now available in 17 markets, TeslaNorth reported Monday from Tesla's FSD tracker.

Mixed reviews for v14.3.13

FSD v14.3.13, first spotted Oct. 20 on software 2026.38.5.10 and later on 2026.38.5.11, carries the same release notes as 14.3.12, FSD Update reported. More than 30% of the fleet is now on it or 14.3 Lite, Tesla Oracle wrote, collecting owner videos that include a Model Y hit during an unprotected left turn, late highway exits and a list of Cybertruck regressions. Other owners called it the steadiest build yet. Tesla's notes mention no regression.

II · Sales & Demand

Norway's best October, and Tesla on top

Registrations more than doubled in Norway, Spain and Sweden; the Netherlands nearly halved.

Tesla registered 1,548 cars in Norway in October, up 130.7% from 671, according to the Norwegian Road Federation (OFV), and was the country's top-selling brand for the month, only 55 cars ahead of Toyota, EV reported. The month was back-loaded: 1,074 new Teslas were registered from Oct. 26 to 31, OFV said, against only 125 in the first 18 days.

In France, the largest of the seven markets, Tesla registered 2,410 cars, up 35.2% but far below September's 9,038. Spain rose 271.2% to 1,459 and Sweden 248% to 463, a year after a union strike hit Swedish sales. Romania had 389, Tesla's best October there, and Denmark 137. The Netherlands was the exception: 336 registrations, down 48% from 645.

Chinese brands gain in Norway

Chinese brands took 22.5% of Norway's new-car registrations in October, the highest monthly share they have reached, OFV said. In France, BYD registered 5,419 cars, more than twice Tesla's total, and in Spain it registered 5,306 (EV). Germany and Britain report later this week.

Ten months in

Tesla remains Norway's best-selling brand this year, with 21,523 registrations through October and a 16.4% share, ahead of Toyota's 14,127, though its total is 3.9% below the same period of 2025, OFV said. French registrations through October more than doubled to 47,609, and Swedish registrations for the first ten months are up 49%, according to data cited by EV. On supply, Giga Berlin, which builds the Model Y for Europe, produced 7,000 cars in a single week in October, a plant record.

III · Energy & Charging

A customs break for the Brookshire Megapack plant

Federal regulators let the Texas site produce Megapacks and inverters under foreign-trade zone rules for 14 months.

The U.S. Foreign-Trade Zones Board authorized Tesla to produce Megapack batteries and Bento inverters at its plant in Brookshire, Texas, near Houston, under Foreign-Trade Zone 84 for 14 months, TeslaNorth reported Tuesday. The decision is dated Oct. 28, and the notice appeared in the Federal Register on Monday. Foreign-trade zone status lets a company defer or reduce customs duties on imported parts used in production.

Tesla filed the request on May 20. Its June proposal listed finished-product duty rates from duty-free to 3.4% and rates on foreign parts from duty-free to 6.5%, according to TeslaNorth. Tesla's Fremont, Livermore and Lathrop sites in California received a similar limited, one-year authorization in August under Foreign-Trade Zone 18. Tesla said in October that Megapacks were almost sold out for 2027.

IV · Optimus & AI

More memory for AI5 and AI6

Musk answered "Wow" to a post on how much memory Tesla's next chips will carry.

"Tesla's upcoming AI5 chip will jump to 96GB, and the AI6 chip is going straight to 144GB," Jun Song posted, comparing them with the 64GB used by Tesla's current FSD computer, and arguing that "better tech" means more memory, not less. Musk replied "Wow" at 4:00 p.m. EST Monday.

Quick Hit

"Far more in taxes." "I have paid far more in taxes than the Tesla loan, which was repaid," Musk wrote at 10:56 p.m. EST Monday, answering a post about government money his companies have received.

Merger would bring control, not the $1 trillion award

If Tesla merged with SpaceX, Musk would gain voting control but would probably not collect the $1 trillion pay package, Reuters reported Tuesday, citing its review of the plan and six executive-compensation experts. In a change in control, the package's operational goals would fall away, but Tesla's market value, about $1.6 trillion now, would still need to reach $2 trillion for any award: $20 billion in shares at that valuation, worth less because the plan pays only for growth since September 2025.

Barclays analysts estimated that if SpaceX acquired Tesla at the companies' current sizes, Musk would own about 37% of the combined company but hold 56% of the voting power, because many of his SpaceX shares carry 10 votes each. Even meeting every goal in the Tesla plan would leave him with about 29% of Tesla's votes. Musk, Tesla and SpaceX did not respond to requests for comment.
