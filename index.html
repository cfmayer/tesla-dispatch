The Morning Picture

Austin's Robotaxi map grows 63% as the Cybertruck narrows to one trim

Tesla's Austin Robotaxi service area grew by about 171 square miles on Monday to roughly 443, a 63% jump that makes it the company's largest unsupervised market again, hours after San Antonio opened, Not a Tesla App reported. Rides in Austin now run from 5 a.m. to 2 a.m. local time, 21 hours a day.

On the order page, Tesla stopped taking orders for the Cybertruck Premium AWD and raised the Cyberbeast by $7,500 to $109,990, leaving one trim for sale. Earthworks are under way at Project Crystal Sun, Tesla's planned $10 billion solar factory near Houston. TSLA rose 5.5% on Monday to $378.05.

I · Full Self-Driving (FSD)

Austin reclaims the biggest Robotaxi zone

The original Robotaxi city adds 171 square miles and an earlier start time; Musk says Cybercab will carry most passenger miles wherever the law allows.

"Bigger Austin service area now live," the Robotaxi account posted at 6:00 a.m. EST Monday. The new geofence pushes east past Pflugerville toward Manor, southeast well beyond Austin-Bergstrom International Airport and the TX-130 toll road, southwest to the TX-45 toll road near Buda and west toward Lakeway, while the northern edge still stops short of Round Rock and Cedar Park, according to Not a Tesla App. The site put the zone at about three times the size of Waymo's Austin area.

Its updated tally of unsupervised markets: Austin 443 square miles, San Antonio 274, Houston 254, Fort Worth 143, Dallas 81, Miami 31, Orlando 26 and Tampa 24. Advertised sizes do not always match where riders can actually be picked up, the site cautioned. Robotaxi hours moved to 5 a.m. to 2 a.m. over the weekend, an hour earlier than before. More than 300 Cybercabs already give rides in Austin, and Dallas is due to become the second Cybercab market this month.

Quick Hit

Busy Sunday. The sightings account CybercabSpotter logged 1,142 Cybercab sightings in Austin on Sunday, Nov 8, 1,061 of them driverless, CyberCab.News reported.

Musk: Cybercab will carry most passenger miles

Carl Hancock (@carlhancock), chief executive of Gravity Forms, asked on Monday how turning Tesla into a taxi company makes sense when the five largest ride-hailing companies have about 16 million drivers and a combined market value of $175 billion. Palmer Luckey (@PalmerLuckey), founder of Anduril, answered at 12:35 a.m. EST Tuesday: "Take that ridesharing revenue ($150B+) and subtract the cost of paying 16,000,000 drivers. That is the bet." Elon Musk replied at 4:44 a.m.: "Cybercab will do a majority of all passenger miles in any jurisdiction where it is not prohibited by law. Obvious to anyone with eyes and a brain" (post).

Lasers to keep robotaxi cameras clear

On a new episode of Jay Leno's Garage, Tesla Vice President of Vehicle Engineering Lars Moravy said focused lasers could clear water from just the patch of windshield in front of the camera, which could suit autonomous cars better than cleaning the whole glass, Not a Tesla App reported. "If you fire them in a focused area, you can clean the sheet of water and create a barrier. When you think about autonomy, it might be a good solution to only clean the area where the camera is," he said, as quoted on X. Tesla's updated patent on laser cleaning of glass was granted on March 31.

Jay Leno rides in the Model Y L

The same 33-minute episode put Leno behind the wheel of the six-seat Model Y L with design chief Franz von Holzhausen and Moravy, using FSD (Supervised); when an ambulance approached from the opposite direction, the car pulled over on its own, TeslaNorth reported. Moravy said the wheelbase grows by about six inches and overall length by about seven, and the executives put the drag coefficient at 0.216.

II · Sales & Demand

Cyberbeast is the last Cybertruck on order

Tesla closes Premium AWD orders and lifts the top trim to $109,990; in China, October retail sales fall to 8,817 as Shanghai ships 87% abroad.

Tesla has stopped taking orders for the Cybertruck Premium AWD, last listed at $92,490, and raised the Cyberbeast's price by $7,500 to $109,990, making it the only Cybertruck that can still be ordered, Not a Tesla App reported. It is the second trim pulled from the configurator in a week, after the Dual Motor AWD was paused at $82,490. The Cyberbeast has risen $10,000 since the start of the year, all of it in the past week.

Tesla's order page now shows Cyberbeast deliveries between March and June 2027, Tesla Oracle reported Tuesday; the estimate had been January to March 2027 on Monday morning, according to Sawyer Merritt. Musk said on Monday that the order rate "far exceeds our production rate" and that many deposits will be returned before the line shuts in about eight months.

China retail sales hit a four-year low

Tesla sold 8,817 vehicles in China in October, down 66.1% from a year earlier and the lowest since July 2022, while Giga Shanghai exported 59,012, a record 87% of its 67,829 wholesale shipments, according to China Passenger Car Association data reported by CnEVPost. Exports rose 66.3% year over year to their second-highest month on record, behind July's 66,330. Tesla's share of China's new energy vehicle (NEV) retail market fell to 0.80% from 2.03% a year earlier.

Through October, Tesla's China retail sales total 389,905, down 15.0%, while Shanghai exports more than doubled to 420,984 and overtook domestic sales for the year. Tesla is offering five-year interest-free loans with a 39,900-yuan minimum down payment on selected Model 3 and Model Y versions for orders through Nov 30, CnEVPost noted.

III · Energy & Charging

Earthworks begin at Project Crystal Sun

Crews are clearing and leveling the 3,000-acre Fort Bend County site for Tesla's vertically integrated solar factory, targeted to start production in 2029.

Tesla has begun earthworks on the roughly 3,000-acre site in Fort Bend County, Texas, for Project Crystal Sun, a planned $10 billion plant that would make the whole solar chain from silicon ingots and wafers to cells and finished modules, Teslarati reported Monday from drone footage by S.E. Robinson, Jr. (@SERobinsonJr). Crews are clearing land, staging equipment and leveling a large part of the property. Tesla has not made a formal groundbreaking announcement.

Filings under Texas's Jobs, Energy, Technology and Innovation Act describe construction from 2026 to 2028 and commercial production from the first quarter of 2029, with about 1,900 jobs in the first year of operations rising to 9,712 by 2033 and a peak of about 1,147 construction jobs, the site said. Fort Bend County approved tax abatements in late October, and the Lamar Consolidated Independent School District approved school-tax limits in September. "Project Crystal Sun," Musk wrote at 10:21 a.m. EST Monday, quote-posting a post on the start of construction.

IV · Chips, Semi & Optimus

Musk: AI6 memory bandwidth unchanged

The chip that will run Optimus loses a third of its memory but keeps its bandwidth; Tesla Semi opens a service center at the Laredo border crossing on Thursday.

Herbert Ong (@herbertong) posted at 8:55 a.m. EST Monday that Tesla has reportedly cut the LPDDR6 memory on its next-generation AI6 chip for Optimus from 216GB to 144GB, a reduction of one third. "Memory bandwidth was held constant though," Musk replied at 10:20 a.m. The change dates to early October, when Musk said AI5 would carry 72GB of LPDDR5, half the earlier plan, and that the cut was the only way to secure enough memory for Optimus production while lowering costs, TrendForce reported. He expects a negligible effect on Optimus performance, since bandwidth matters more than capacity.

A Semi service center in Laredo

Tesla is opening a Semi Service Center at 13505 Regional Drive in Laredo, Texas, with an invitation-only celebration on Thursday, Nov 12, from 4 p.m. to 7 p.m. local time, including site tours and Semi ride-alongs, TeslaNorth reported from Tesla's event listing. Tesla began volume Semi production in September at its Sparks, Nevada, plant, which is designed for 50,000 trucks a year, and Forum Mobility opened two Megawatt Charging Standard stalls for the Semi at the Port of Long Beach on Nov 5.
