The Morning Picture

Tesla trades car referrals for FSD referrals

Tesla on Thursday evening introduced its first referral program for FSD (Supervised) in the United States and ended vehicle referrals there. An owner who subscribes to FSD (Supervised) or bought it outright can share a link that gives an eligible Tesla owner a one-month trial; if that person subscribes during the trial, a subscriber earns a free month and an outright buyer earns a $99 Tesla Credit, Sawyer Merritt posted at 9:01 p.m. EDT, quoting the program terms. Benefits can be earned up to 12 times per calendar year.

The FSD program replaces the $250 vehicle referral credit in the U.S., EV reported, with a lifetime cap of 48 referrals per account; it requires version 4.62.0 or later of the Tesla app, according to TeslaNorth. "Tesla has ended vehicle referrals in the U.S.," Not a Tesla App posted at 9:07 p.m. About an hour after the launch, Merritt wrote that he had already hit the 12-referral limit: "You have sent 12 referrals. Refer again on January 1st."

I · Full Self-Driving (FSD)

Robotaxi now runs until 2 a.m.

The service added longer hours, a rear-view camera feed, trip sharing, Calm Mode and Grok on its Model Y fleet.

"Operating hours are now 6am - 2am," the Robotaxi account posted at 12:49 a.m. EDT Friday. New features include a rear-view camera feed riders can watch during the trip, trip details that can be shared with a friend, a redesigned Live Activity, and Calm Mode, which "dims the screen & sets cabin lighting to a relaxing state." Grok is "now available on our Model Y vehicles." "Until bar closing time now!" Tesla's Ashok Elluswamy wrote at 1:48 a.m., quoting the post.

The same update listed accessibility changes: an improved vehicle-locating arrow in the app, haptic feedback to help riders find their car, and music control through a screen reader (Robotaxi).

"Designed for everyone from day 1"

"Last week, we hosted Accessibility Day with members of the disabled community – their feedback goes directly into all Robotaxi products, including Cybercab and future products," the Robotaxi account posted at 1:43 p.m. EDT Thursday. Tesla reposted a rider, Rob Loken, who wrote: "Totally blind, first ride in Robotaxi, the app was totally accessible, the ride accessible, the drop-off accessible, it felt unusually normal!"

Quick Hit

Quiet Mode. Cybercab has a new "Quiet Mode" that removes most movement from its screen, Teslarati posted at 10:21 p.m. EDT Thursday.

Update 2026.38.6 arrives

Tesla has released software update 2026.38.6, Not a Tesla App posted at 3:11 p.m. EDT Thursday. Separately, owners using third-party vehicle-to-load adapters from Elejoy and others report that power cuts out within minutes after update 2026.38.5.1, as Tesla expands its own PowerShare V2L, the site reported at 12:17 p.m.

Quick Hit

"Much appreciated." Musk replied at 9:02 a.m. EDT Thursday to Matteo Salvini, Italy's deputy prime minister, who had said Italy "supports a swift EU approval of Tesla Assisted Driving."

II · Sales & Demand

Model Y RWD sets a Green NCAP low

The crossover posted the lowest life-cycle emissions the program has published, and the Semi won a Top Gear award.

"At 95 g CO₂-eq/km, Model Y RWD has the lowest life-cycle emissions Green NCAP has ever published," Tesla Europe posted at 9:21 a.m. EDT Thursday. Green NCAP is the European program that rates cars' environmental performance.

Semi takes a Top Gear tech award

The Tesla Semi won Top Gear's 2027 Tech Award, with the magazine saying the electric truck shows what Tesla can do at its best, Not a Tesla App reported at 3:32 p.m. EDT Thursday. Top Gear's Supercar of the Year went to the Corvette ZR1X rather than the new Roadster, the site said.

Detroit rallies

General Motors shares rose 3.93% on Thursday and Ford gained 3.43%, while Rivian fell 1.89%, according to Yahoo Finance data. Over five sessions GM is up 11.02%, against Tesla's 7.67%.

III · Energy & Charging

Powerwall learns to run the heat pump

Heat Pump Optimization reaches Powerwall 3 in Europe, and six new Supercharger sites added 48 stalls.

"Your heat pump is one of the biggest electrical loads in your home," Tesla Energy posted at 11:25 p.m. EDT Thursday. "Heat Pump Optimization allows your Powerwall to boost heating when there's surplus solar or energy is inexpensive, helping you save money when rates are high." The feature is available with Powerwall 3 and Powerwall 3P in Europe.

Six sites, 48 stalls

Tesla Charging announced Zalaegerszeg, Hungary, eight stalls (11:14 a.m. EDT Thursday); Érd, Hungary, eight (1:27 p.m.); East Wenatchee, Washington, a six-stall site under its Supercharger for Business program (4:19 p.m.); Albany, Oregon, 16 (6:33 p.m.); Tai Koo, Hong Kong, four (10:34 p.m.); and Edogawa-ku Rinkaicho, Japan, six (11:04 p.m.).

IV · Board & Shareholders

The board asks for a vote for Musk, Denholm and Straubel

A new shareholder letter makes the case for the three Class I directors before the Dec. 3 meeting.

"We have all the building blocks necessary to propel us into our next phase of transformational growth, but we must keep the leadership that has proven key to Tesla's success," Tesla's board wrote in a letter to shareholders filed with the SEC on Thursday (DEFA14A). It asks shareholders to vote "FOR ALL proposals on this year's ballot — most importantly, the re-election of our three Class I Directors": Elon Musk, Robyn Denholm and JB Straubel. The annual meeting is Dec. 3 at 4:00 p.m. EST.

The letter says FSD (Supervised) has reached "1.74 million paid customers globally with a record ~75% attach rate for new vehicle deliveries in North America as of Q3 2026," that trailing twelve-month revenue has grown to "$100+ billion," and that Tesla has delivered "more than 10 million vehicles to date." It describes Terafab as the project "which will secure the supply of critical components as we scale for massive capacity" (filing; Sawyer Merritt posted the letter at 5:17 p.m. EDT).

"The Terafab project is going well"

Musk posted that line at 1:12 a.m. EDT Friday, quoting a summary of a report by Korea's The Elec. In that opinion piece, The Elec wrote that Tesla and SpaceX procurement staff visited about 10 Korean supplier booths at SEMICON West and that more than half of those companies signed non-disclosure agreements. "Even when we send inquiries in the early morning hours in the U.S., we receive responses within 30 minutes to an hour," one supplier told the paper, and an overseas equipment executive said: "I've never seen this level of execution speed in the semiconductor industry." The Elec put the project's target at 1 million wafers a month within 10 years.

Quick Hit

"Very high too." To a list of expected four-year profit growth topped by SpaceX, Musk replied at 11:15 p.m. EDT Thursday: "Tesla will be very high too."
