The Morning Picture

Musk plans a Model Y with no steering wheel

Tesla plans an autonomous version of the Model Y without a steering wheel or pedals for groups of six or seven people, Elon Musk told Andrew Ross Sorkin at The New York Times' DealBook Summit on Wednesday, joining by video from Shenzhen, China, EV (eletric-vehicles.com) reported. "What we plan to do is have an autonomous version of Model Y that wouldn't have steering wheels and pedals and would be good for transporting groups of six or seven people," he said, adding that the two-seat Cybercab "seats 2 people, which covers 90% of all passenger miles." Musk gave no timeline and did not say whether the vehicle would be sold to customers, run in the Robotaxi fleet or both.

In Shenzhen, Musk said Tesla will keep increasing production in China. Tesla also answered the proxy advisers ISS and Glass Lewis, which oppose re-electing Board Chair Robyn Denholm, in a letter filed with the SEC, and the first Cybercab specification page surfaced with a 279-mile range. TSLA rose 0.9% to $383.72 on Wednesday.

I · Full Self-Driving (FSD)

Cybercab spec page lists a 279-mile range

Dallas Cybercab rides look close; Europe gets stricter driver monitoring, and France asked to keep its FSD correspondence secret.

Tesla rates the Cybercab at 279 miles of EPA-estimated range and says Supercharging adds 123 miles in 15 minutes, according to a specifications page on the order site shown to customers invited to buy the two-seater for personal use, EV reported on Thursday. It is the first official range figure, about 5% below the roughly 293 miles implied by the car's EPA certification documents. A footnote bases the charging figure on efficiency at a constant 65 mph. The page lists a length of 176.2 inches; the production car has no onboard charger and charges only at NACS direct-current fast chargers, including Superchargers. Invited buyers pay $29,990 plus a required $299 monthly autonomy subscription.

Dallas Cybercab rides look close

Tesla's Robotaxi account posted "Just leaving this here" at 11:44 a.m. EST Wednesday with a photo of the Dallas skyline, including the green outline of Bank of America Plaza, reflected in a Cybercab window, Drive Tesla reported. On Tesla's October earnings call, AI chief Ashok Elluswamy said "next month, we'll be launching in Dallas," and a video over the weekend showed more than 50 Cybercabs parked at a Dallas lot, Teslarati noted. Tesla has not announced a start date. Public Cybercab rides have been limited to Austin since September; Model Y robotaxis in Dallas have run with no one in the driver's seat around the clock since Nov 11.

A robotaxi ride during service visits

Tesla owners in Austin who bring cars in for service are getting in-app prompts headed "Try Robotaxi During Your Service Visit," offering an autonomous ride to their next destination in place of loaners or rideshare credits, Teslarati reported. Separately, iPhone app 4.63.0 adds shared login with the Robotaxi app, the only item in its release notes, TeslaNorth reported.

Quick Hit

A flush Starlink cover. Drone images of Giga Texas on Nov 18 show rows of production Cybercabs with a flush-mounted Starlink terminal on the rear hatch that blends into the body lines more cleanly than the panel shown in July and August, Teslarati reported.

Stricter driver monitoring in Europe

The Dutch vehicle authority RDW approved a new update to Tesla Assisted Driving, the name Tesla now uses for FSD (Supervised) in Europe, for Hardware 4 cars, Drive Tesla reported. Drivers get attention warnings sooner and a "full strike" if they are inattentive while the car performs a maneuver, and above 130 km/h they must keep their hands on the wheel. Setting a maximum speed above the one the system shows now needs confirmation with the scroll wheel, and the speed-limit icon pulses red if the car exceeds it. The RDW tested the update on a track and public roads. Ten countries have adopted the Dutch approval, most recently Finland, and an EU-wide vote is possible in December. The update follows a cut in the system's speed margin from 50% to 10% two weeks earlier; in the US, drivers may still go hands-free at any speed, Electrek noted.

France asked for secrecy on FSD files

Bulgaria's vehicle regulator, the Executive Agency Automobile Administration, refused a freedom-of-information request for its correspondence on FSD (Supervised) on Nov 13, citing French authorities who "explicitly stated that all correspondence regarding the Tesla Full Self-Driving (Supervised) system should be treated as confidential," EV reported from the decision. The software still needs a vote by the European Commission's Technical Committee on Motor Vehicles (TCMV) for approval across the bloc.

II · Sales & Production

Musk: China output will keep rising

Shanghai exports have doubled this year as local sales fall; Berlin adds weekend shifts, and the Model Y L may take over the line.

Musk told Ren Hongbin, chairman of the China Council for the Promotion of International Trade and chair of the APEC CEO Summit 2026, that Tesla will continue to increase production in China, according to a report by Yuyuan Tantian, a media brand affiliated with China Central Television, cited by CnEVPost. It was his first public visit to Shenzhen; he arrived on Nov 18 with a US delegation for APEC Economic Leaders' Week.

Giga Shanghai delivered 810,889 vehicles from January to October, up 21.42% from a year earlier. Tesla's retail sales in China fell 15.00% to 389,905 over the same period while exports from Shanghai rose 101.28% to 420,984. In October, domestic retail sales fell 66.10% to 8,817 and exports of 59,012 made up a record 87% of the plant's 67,829 wholesale sales, CnEVPost said.

Extra weekend shifts at Giga Berlin

Tesla has ordered up to six extra Friday-night and Saturday-morning shifts for vehicle-production staff at Giga Berlin before early December, the first two this weekend and the other four conditional, after the plant missed its target of 7,500 Model Ys a week by mid-October, Drive Tesla reported, citing Handelsblatt. The factory built 7,000 in one week in mid-October. Management cited Model Y demand.

The Model Y L could become the only Model Y

"I think we probably need to change all of the Model Y production to the Y L because it's what most people seem to want," Musk said at DealBook, calling the longer six-seater "certainly our most popular car," EV reported. In the US it sells as a single all-wheel-drive version at $61,990 with three rows and an EPA-rated 325 miles, built in Austin. Berlin does not build it, and Musk gave no sign that a switch had been decided.

Quick Hit

Manitoba must name a decision-maker. A Manitoba judge gave the province 10 days to file a sworn record showing who decided to drop Tesla from its EV rebate program in March 2025 and why; Tesla vehicles remain ineligible for the $4,000 rebate, Drive Tesla reported.

III · Energy, Charging & Semi

Tesla weighs a $3.1 billion polysilicon refinery

A Texas filing outlines a plant for solar-grade silicon; HMD Trucking takes its first 50 Semis.

Tesla is considering a $3.1 billion refinery near Robstown in Nueces County, Texas, that would turn ultra-pure trichlorosilane into solar-grade polysilicon for high-efficiency N-type solar cells, Drive Tesla reported from a new filing. Construction would start in 2027, with commercial operation targeted for 2030. Tesla says China supplies more than 90% of the world's solar-grade polysilicon. The company will build in Texas only if it wins a deal under the state's JETI program, which limits the property value used for local school-district taxes. The filing estimates an average of 1,770 jobs a year across Texas during construction and 260 refinery employees in 2030, rising to 515 by 2032.

Quick Hit

Powerwalls on the grid. "Powerwall owners dispatch power to stabilize the grid. One of 100+ Tesla VPP Programs worldwide," Tesla Energy posted at 1:07 p.m. EST Wednesday, quoting a Maine owner's post about 168 homes supporting the grid there.

50 Semis for HMD Trucking

Chicago-area carrier HMD Trucking has received its first 50 Tesla Semis, a transportation video creator reported on LinkedIn on Nov 18 after visiting Tesla's six-stall Megacharger in Hodgkins, Illinois, TeslaNorth reported. HMD says its first truck arrived on Nov 2 and was the first Semi registered in Illinois, and that a summer test over 4,000 miles averaged 1.74 kWh per mile.

With Mesquite, Texas, open since Tuesday, Tesla's map lists seven public Megacharger sites with 44 stalls in four states, against about 37 sites shown for 2026 in Tesla's Q4 2025 shareholder report, Electrek counted.

Plug & Charge for Teslas at IONNA

The charging network IONNA enabled Plug & Charge for Tesla vehicles across its network on Wednesday, so drivers who link a car and card in its Presto app can plug in and charge without tapping or swiping, Electrek reported. Its new 22-bay flagship in Westminster, California, has 12 CCS and 10 NACS connectors.

Google Maps in the iPhone app

Tesla app 4.63.0 swaps Apple Maps for Google Maps on iPhone, matching Android and smoothing Supercharger site maps, Not a Tesla App reported. Code in the same version includes "Precondition Battery" and "Stop Preconditioning" buttons for warming the pack before fast charging, a feature that is not live yet, Not a Tesla App found.

IV · Terafab, Roadster & Merger Talk

Musk wants "hardcore engineers" for Terafab

A first test chip is targeted for around June 2027; the Roadster goes on show in Los Angeles on Friday.

"Looking for hardcore engineers to join Terafab!"

Elon Musk (@elonmusk), 8:38 p.m. EST Wednesday

Musk's post quoted a transcript of his DealBook remarks on Terafab, the chip plant Tesla and SpaceX are building. "We expect to have the research fab in operation and hopefully make our first test chip around June of next year," he said, with volume production "towards the end of 2028, at least for phase one of Terafab." He said he expects the plant "to ultimately be 100 million square feet," which would make it the largest building of any kind on Earth, according to the transcript he shared.

"Probably" better together

Asked about combining Tesla and SpaceX, Musk said: "You don't need them to be together, but would they be better together? I mean, probably yes," adding that "these require shareholder votes, which may or may not succeed, at least in the case of Tesla," EV reported. On his pay: "I don't think I should be compensated with any kind of award just for doing a merger. That would be unreasonable and not right." Tesla and SpaceX have each formed special committees of independent directors to weigh a merger, Musk said in October.

The Roadster heads to the LA Auto Show

The LA Auto Show runs Nov 20 to 29 at the Los Angeles Convention Center, with Tesla among the exhibitors and the Model Y L among the listed highlights, according to the show's press release. The show teased a "cover pull" with the tag #teslaroadster in a post on X, signaling that the new Roadster will be on display.

A tuner's CyberSUV pitch

Unplugged Performance expanded its offer to keep the Cybertruck line alive after Tesla ends production in summer 2027: a new company would rebuild the line near Giga Texas, add a three-row CyberSUV, and pay Tesla a license fee, parts margin and FSD revenue plus a free stake, Not a Tesla App reported.

V · SEC Filings

Tesla answers ISS and Glass Lewis

A five-page letter defends Board Chair Robyn Denholm two weeks before the Dec 3 annual meeting.

Tesla filed additional proxy materials (DEFA14A) at 5:15 p.m. EST Wednesday with an updated VoteTesla.com site carrying a letter titled "A Response to ISS and Glass Lewis," according to the filing. The two proxy advisers recommend voting against re-electing Denholm; the letter calls their recommendations "myopic, misguided and based on misconceptions" and says they "own no Tesla shares."

The letter credits Denholm, chair since 2018, with growth in Tesla's market value from about $60 billion to more than $1.5 trillion and a total shareholder return of about 1,581% through Nov 13. It says FSD (Supervised) had 1.74 million paid customers worldwide, with a record attach rate of about 75% for new vehicles delivered in North America in the third quarter of 2026.

The two claims Tesla disputes

ISS and Glass Lewis say the board was "insufficiently responsive" to a majority-supported 2025 shareholder proposal to declassify the board. Tesla replies that only 39% of outstanding shares backed its own binding proposal to remove supermajority voting requirements in 2025, short of the 66.67% needed. Glass Lewis also cites low board gender diversity; Tesla calls it ironic to oppose re-electing a woman on those grounds and notes that Kathleen Wilson-Thompson, whom Glass Lewis opposed last year, won more than 78% of votes cast. The letter asks holders to vote for Denholm, Musk and JB Straubel and for all proposals. "Remember to cast your Tesla votes!" Musk posted at 2:39 a.m. EST Thursday.
