The Morning Picture

Tesla picks Berlin for a second Cybercab factory

Tesla will spend about €3 billion ($3.3 billion) on a new production hall at its plant in Grünheide, outside Berlin, designed to build up to 1 million Cybercabs a year, with construction planned for the first half of 2027 and production in 2029, EV (eletric-vehicles.com) reported on Monday, citing a company statement carried by the German news agency dpa. It is the first time Tesla has given a timeline for building the two-seat robotaxi outside the United States.

Later on Monday, Elon Musk said trials letting owners put their own cars on the Robotaxi network could start next month, and Robotaxi riders can now pay with X Money. Tesla's Megapack team has a reveal planned for today at the RE+ energy show in Las Vegas. TSLA fell 2.7% to $379.74 on Monday as the Nasdaq 100 dropped 2.7%.

I · Full Self-Driving (FSD)

Owners' cars could join Robotaxi in trials next month

Musk says owner trials may start in December; riders can now pay with X Money, and the Miami service area nearly doubles.

An Austin ride-hailing driver posting as @apostlejohn wrote at 1:56 p.m. EST Monday that he drives for Uber using FSD exclusively, keeps 49% to 63% of each passenger fare, and would "rather send my Model Y off to work for Robotaxi without me." Musk answered three hours later:

"Coming soon. If things well, may start trials next month. Much better to manage a fleet of cars than have to drive just one car by yourself!"

Elon Musk (@elonmusk), replying to @apostlejohn, Mon Nov 16, 4:57 p.m. EST

Musk did not say where a trial would run, which owners could take part or how fares would be split, TeslaNorth noted. The personal-use Cybercab terms published last week bar putting the car on Robotaxi or other ride-hailing networks unless Tesla authorizes it. JPMorgan analyst Rajat Gupta wrote on Sunday that he expects customer-owned cars running unsupervised FSD to become "another, capital-light source of supply" for the network starting in the second quarter of 2027, Yahoo Finance reported. He counted 1,306 registered Tesla robotaxis in Texas, against 1,321 for Alphabet's Waymo.

Robotaxi riders can pay with X Money

"You can now link your @XMoney to the Robotaxi app," the Robotaxi account posted at 2:59 p.m. EST Monday. Riders add it under Payment in the app, which hands off to the X app to confirm, and X Money says payments can earn up to 3% cashback, Drive Tesla reported. X Money is available only in the United States.

Miami's service area nearly doubles

The Miami expansion Robotaxi announced early Monday added about 28 square miles, a 92.4% increase to about 59 square miles, by the measurement of the independent Robotaxi Tracker, Not a Tesla App reported. The new boundary runs east to Downtown Miami, Brickell and Biscayne Bay and keeps the airport, Doral and Coral Gables; Miami Beach is still outside it. Tesla did not publish an area figure.

Quick Hit

Mercedes point-to-point. Mercedes-Benz will begin a nationwide US rollout of MB.DRIVE ASSIST PRO, a Level 2 system that steers, turns and stops at lights along a navigation route, in 2027, plus hands-free highway driving up to 85 mph from spring 2027; select California owners get early access in December, the company said on Monday.

What's inside software 2026.44.1

The firmware researcher known as green (@greentheonly) posted at 12:39 a.m. EST Tuesday that Tesla's 2026.44.1 build now calls the car's AI computer the "SI Computer" in vehicle properties. He listed alerts when the price at the next charging stop goes up or down, Grok chat transcripts that can be read later in the Grok phone app, a smart charging-schedule mode, battery-preconditioning changes, Supercharger queue tweaks and a Linux kernel update. Asked which FSD build it carries, he replied "14.3.13 & 14.3 lite," TeslaNorth reported. Tesla has not released the update or its notes.

Barclays calls round-the-clock rides a proof point

Barclays analyst Dan Levy reiterated an Overweight rating and $450 target on Monday, saying last week's move to 24-hour Robotaxi service in every city, using cameras alone, is a proof point of Tesla's improving self-driving software, Investing.com reported.

II · Sales & Demand

Kia passes Tesla in South Korean EV sales

Tesla's Korean registrations have doubled this year, but Kia's electric sales have more than doubled.

Kia sold 120,360 battery-electric vehicles in South Korea from January through Nov 9, more than double a year earlier, while Tesla, the country's best-selling imported brand, registered 98,313 cars from January to October, up 105.0%, according to the Korea Automobile Importers and Distributors Association, EV reported on Monday. The two totals cover slightly different periods, leaving Tesla within reach of 100,000 for the year.

Tesla registered 9,165 cars in Korea in October, more than double a year earlier but 25.9% fewer than in September, a drop the Yonhap news agency attributed to supply shortages. BYD registered 21,731 cars in the first ten months, up 473.2%. Imported EV registrations reached 147,006, 47.3% of all imported vehicles sold in the country.

III · Energy & Charging

Megapack team teases a reveal at RE+ today

"Transformation loading..." at booth N726; a California Supercharger opens early by talking to the grid, and six new sites post since Monday.

Tesla's Megapack account shared a dark teaser image on Monday reading "Transformation loading..." above the date Nov 17 and booth number N726, putting a reveal at the RE+ renewable-energy show in Las Vegas today, TeslaNorth reported. Tesla has not named the product or given specifications. Tesla deployed 13.7 gigawatt-hours (GWh) of battery storage in the third quarter, up 10% from a year earlier, and has started production at its Texas Megafactory, which is planned for 50 GWh a year.

A Supercharger that opens before the grid upgrade

"Our Cloverdale, CA Supercharger is a PG&E Flex Connect site, talking to the grid over IEEE 2030.5 to open faster," Tesla Charging posted at 8:24 p.m. EST Monday. Under the Pacific Gas and Electric program, the utility sends a day-ahead schedule of hourly power limits and the site keeps its draw within them while permanent upgrades are built, TeslaNorth explained. Tesla lists eight stalls at up to 500 kilowatts (kW), open 24 hours, at Penny Faire Plaza.

Six more sites since Monday

Tesla Charging also announced Murfreesboro, Tennessee, Elam Road with 24 stalls (post); Depew, New York, with 12 (post); Rochester, New York, with 12 (post); Stafford Township, New Jersey, with 8 (post); and El Dorado Hills, California, Vine Street with 16 (post). With Cloverdale, that is six sites and 80 stalls in a day.

Quebec's first full V4 site takes shape

An eight-stall Supercharger under construction at the A&W on Chemin Gascon in Terrebonne will use both V4 posts and V4 power cabinets, making it Quebec's first site capable of up to 500 kW rather than the usual 325 kW, the tracker MarcoRP reported, according to Drive Tesla. Another eight V3 stalls are starting construction in Repentigny, off Autoroute 40.

Quick Hit

IONNA Autocharge. Tesla owners can now start charging at IONNA stations in the United States just by plugging in, after a one-time vehicle check in the Presto Charging app; the first Autocharge session is free, Drive Tesla reported.

Installers tour the Powerwall line

Tesla Certified Installers from around the world visited Giga Nevada to see Powerwall production, Tesla Energy posted at 5:18 p.m. EST Monday: "Built in America, installed across the world."

IV · Factories & Production

A hall more than twice the size of the plant

The Berlin investment covers a new building, a second wastewater plant and hundreds of immediate hires; Europe still caps driverless models at 1,500 a year.

"If you ever wondered, what we would be doing at Giga Berlin with all the land that we have… We are expanding! Cybercab production is coming to Giga Berlin! Today we announced a first investment of around € 3b in a new manufacturing building with an anticpated start of production in 2029! It will have the most advanced technology of any Tesla factory! And we are hiring"

André Thierig (@AndrThie), plant manager, Giga Berlin, post, Mon Nov 16, 11:19 a.m. EST

The €3 billion covers the production hall north of the existing plant, ancillary buildings and a second wastewater treatment plant, Electrek reported from Tesla's statement. Tesla plans to file the building application before the end of the year. Public broadcaster rbb reported in October that the hall would cover about 536,000 square meters, against about 219,000 for the current building, EV noted. Tesla called the €3 billion a first step and is hiring several hundred people for construction and production planning now.

"Three billion euros for Grünheide, that is absolutely huge," Brandenburg's economy minister, Martina Klement, said, according to the Berlin daily Tagesspiegel, as cited by EV. The plant builds only the Model Y. It made a record 7,000 in one week in October and is aiming for 7,500 a week, and its workforce stood at 12,600 that month, Tesla said, according to dpa.

From pause to expansion

Tesla put the larger Berlin build-out on hold in 2024. The plant built about 202,000 vehicles in 2025, running at 54% of capacity, though net profit rose to €77.1 million from €56.8 million, according to Tesla Manufacturing Brandenburg's annual report, EV reported. Brandenburg granted a first partial permit for the 1-million-vehicle expansion in October 2024, using only land Tesla already owns. Tesla is also adding 4680 battery cell production at the site, with 18 GWh of annual capacity planned, Electrek reported.

Europe's limit on driverless cars

Under current EU rules, a fully driverless vehicle type can be approved only as a "small series" of up to 1,500 registrations a year across the bloc, and Tesla has no European approval for unsupervised driving; its supervised system, sold there as Tesla Assisted Driving, is approved in the Netherlands and recognized by other EU countries, Electrek reported. In Texas, where the Cybercab is built today, Tesla lists capacity of more than 125,000 a year, EV noted.

The United States has its own limit: the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) opened a probe in September into how Tesla concluded a car with no steering wheel or pedals meets federal safety standards, and JPMorgan warns an audit could cap Cybercab deployment at 2,500 a year under a federal exemption, Yahoo Finance reported.

Musk recalls the 2018 weld cull

An X user (@stark0xbt) relayed on Monday the account by investor and former Tesla director Antonio Gracias of a 3 a.m. night in the body shop in 2018, when Tesla was, by Musk's own account, about a month from bankruptcy. Musk replied at 2:31 a.m. EST Tuesday: "I had one of our best structural engineers mark spot welds for deletion on one side of the body-in-white and I did the other (the welds are symmetric). Our judgments matched exactly, apart from debate about a few, which was settled quickly. If we had been wrong, it would have resulted in a recall. We were not."

Unplugged pitches a CyberSUV company

Tesla aftermarket specialist Unplugged Performance proposed on Nov 15 a new company that would take over the Cybertruck line when it leaves Giga Texas, build a three-row CyberSUV alongside the pickup and give Tesla a free equity stake, a per-vehicle license fee and approval over leadership, specifications and brand, TeslaNorth reported. Tesla has not announced any agreement, and Unplugged, which has never run a high-volume vehicle line, has not named committed investors.

V · SEC Filings

The board asks shareholders to vote "FOR" everything

A new proxy filing carries a board letter for the Dec 3 annual meeting; Bank of America added Tesla shares in the third quarter.

Tesla filed additional proxy materials (DEFA14A) at 5:15 p.m. EST Monday containing a letter the board sent shareholders on Nov 14 and an updated VoteTesla.com site, according to the filing. The letter asks holders to vote "FOR" the re-election of the Class I directors and all proposals at the annual meeting on Dec 3 at 3:00 p.m. Central, 4:00 p.m. EST. "Being a Tesla shareholder is not like holding stock in any other company. It is a statement of belief," the board wrote, citing a total shareholder return of about 343 times since the initial public offering. The filing names the nine directors (Elon Musk, Robyn Denholm, Ira Ehrenpreis, Joe Gebbia, Jack Hartung, James Murdoch, Kimbal Musk, JB Straubel and Kathleen Wilson-Thompson) and executives Vaibhav Taneja and Tom Zhu as participants in the solicitation. Shareholders can vote by phone, mail, online or QR code; the proxy solicitor is Innisfree M&A.

Bank of America adds to its stake

Bank of America raised its Tesla holding 5.9% in the third quarter to 19,192,513 shares from 18,117,418, while the stake's value fell to $6.81 billion from $7.62 billion, according to its 13F filed on Monday, EV reported. Tesla was the bank's 40th-largest holding on Sep 30, down from 34th in June.
