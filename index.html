The Morning Picture

Model Y rules Europe's September charts, Slovakia readies Full Self-Driving, and Dan Ives returns with a $500 target

Wednesday's news came mostly from Europe. Tesla said the Model Y was the best-selling passenger car in all five Nordic countries in September, and German registration data showed it outsold the Volkswagen Golf by about 22%. Slovakia's transport minister said the country would approve Full Self-Driving (FSD) (Supervised) "within days," which would make it the ninth European Union (EU) country to do so, as a new FSD build, v14.3.11, began reaching owners. Tesla will also show a Cybercab and an Optimus robot at the Paris Motor Show next week.

On Wall Street, Dan Ives made Tesla his first call at his new firm, Yorkville Ives, with an Outperform rating and a $500 price target, and UBS raised its target to $391. The stock still slipped 0.75% to $377.81 on light volume, ending a three-day winning streak. In energy, more than 85,000 California Powerwalls sent over 600 megawatts (MW) to the grid in a record virtual power plant event, and the first Semi Megacharger on the East Coast opened near Atlanta. Tesla has filed nothing new with the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) since Wednesday morning; third-quarter results arrive October 21.

I · Full Self-Driving (FSD)

A new build for every car, and a ninth EU country in sight

FSD v14.3.11 and v14.3 Lite began rolling out together, Slovakia said it will recognize the Dutch approval, and the Robotaxi fleet kept growing in Texas.

Software: v14.3.11 and v14.3 Lite

Tesla began pushing software update 2026.33.5 to early-access owners in the US late Tuesday, Drive Tesla reported. Newer AI4 (Hardware 4) cars get FSD (Supervised) v14.3.11, and older Hardware 3 (HW3) cars get v14.3 Lite, a distilled version of the v14 model that Tesla first brought to HW3 in June. The FSD release notes for v14.3.11 are the same as v14.3.10, which suggests bug fixes rather than a new model. The update does add Automatic Collision Evasion on AI4 cars: it can engage FSD while the car is being driven manually if it detects an impending collision. Not a Tesla App lists the release date as October 7.

"FSD 14.3 LITE coming in to my 2018 Model 3"
Tesla Raj · @tesla_raj · post, Wed 5:34 p.m. EDT

Approvals: Slovakia says "within days"

In a LinkedIn video on Wednesday, Transport Minister Jozef Ráž said Slovakia expects to approve FSD (Supervised) within days, Reuters reported. Slovakia will send a letter recognizing the provisional approval that the Netherlands' vehicle authority, the RDW, granted on April 10.

"Slovakia expects to approve @Tesla's FSD (Supervised) within days, Transport Minister Jozef Raz said, which would make it the ninth European Union country to approve FSD. "Data shows that these cars reduce the accident rate and that's exactly what we're aiming for.""
Sawyer Merritt · @SawyerMerritt · post, Thu 7:08 a.m. EDT

An EU-wide approval needs a qualified majority in the Technical Committee on Motor Vehicles (TCMV): at least 15 of 27 member states representing 65% of the bloc's population. The committee's October 6 meeting listed only a "continuation of discussions" on the Dutch request, and the next opportunity for a vote is December, NotATeslaKiller reported. Germany, France, Italy and Spain have not recognized the Dutch approval. Germany's transport ministry said on October 6 it had reached an understanding with Tesla that the system may exceed the speed limit "by a maximum of 10%," and Sweden has said it would vote against unless Tesla removes its speed-offset function (EV). Tesla Europe opened Wednesday with a winter-testing video from Switzerland: "Winter is coming / FSD Supervised is ready."

Paris: Cybercab on the stand, FSD rides outside

Tesla will show a Cybercab and an Optimus Gen 2 humanoid robot at the Mondial de l'Auto, October 12–18 (Hall 7.1, stand C25), according to Tesla Mag. It also plans supervised FSD demonstration drives in six Model 3s, TeslAnt reports, even though France has not approved the system.

Robotaxi and Cybercab

Tesla's Robotaxi service runs in Austin, Dallas, Houston, Miami, Orlando and Tampa, according to Tesla's Robotaxi page; paid Cybercab rides are offered only in Austin. Texas Department of Motor Vehicles records showed 169 Cybercabs authorized for commercial use as of October 2, up from 45 at the Austin launch on September 3, CNBC reported. In Houston, where Cybercabs are testing but not yet carrying the public, drone footage suggested 190 to 200 of them at Tesla's staging lot, Drive Tesla reported Wednesday, citing an estimate by longtime observer S.E. Robinson Jr.

II · Sales & Demand

Model Y tops the charts from Oslo to Berlin

September registration data, published this week, show Tesla's best month in Germany since 2022 and a clean sweep of the Nordics.

8,038Model Y registrations in Germany in September, No. 1 of all models; the VW Golf had 6,580 (KBA data)
12,552Tesla cars registered in Germany in September, up 268.7% from 3,404 a year earlier
4,810Model Y registrations in Norway, 25.68% of all new passenger cars (OFV via electrive)
3 in 4Norwegian Tesla owners who buy another Tesla as their next car, per Tesla Europe

Tesla Europe, Middle East & Africa (@teslaeurope) posted at 9:22 a.m. EDT Wednesday that the Model Y was the best-selling car across the Nordic countries in September. Tesla was also the top-selling brand overall in Denmark, Iceland and Norway, TeslaNorth reported.

"3 in 4 Norwegian Tesla owners make their next car another Tesla. We love ya too"
Tesla Europe, Middle East & Africa · @teslaeurope · post, Wed 10:41 a.m. EDT (flag emoji omitted)

Germany was the bigger surprise. Data from the Kraftfahrt-Bundesamt (KBA), Germany's federal motor transport authority, put the Model Y first among all models with 8,038 registrations and the Model 3 sixth with 4,512, Best Selling Cars Blog reported. It is only the second time the Model Y has led Germany's overall chart, after September 2022. Tesla's 12,552 German registrations gave it a 4.9% share in a market where battery-electric cars reached 34.5% of sales, per NotATeslaKiller.

In Norway, electrive counted 4,935 Tesla registrations and a 26.3% brand share, down from 33.7% a year earlier.

III · Energy & Charging

Powerwalls set a grid record; Semi charging reaches the East

Home batteries acted like a power plant on Tuesday, and the heavy-truck charging network finally left California.

“
Less than a month later, a new record for the largest Powerwall VPP event. On Tuesday, 85,000+ Powerwall owners across California dispatched more than 600 MW to the grid, helping to meet peak demand
Tesla Energy · @teslaenergy · Thursday, October 8, 12:59 p.m. EDT

A virtual power plant (VPP) pools home batteries so a utility can draw on them at peak hours. Sawyer Merritt (@SawyerMerritt) put the figures at 85,774 homes delivering 601 MW, with the whole fleet peaking at a record 634 MW, and added that across ten California events in 2026, Powerwall owners have sent 5 gigawatt-hours (GWh) back to the grid.

First East Coast Megacharger

Tesla Charging (@TeslaCharging) announced "New Tesla Megacharger: Conley, GA (8 stalls)" at 8:55 p.m. EDT Wednesday. The site, just outside Atlanta, is the first for the Semi truck on the East Coast; each stall can deliver up to 1.2 MW (1,200 kilowatts), Sawyer Merritt noted.

Every new Tesla as a home battery

On Tuesday, Tesla Energy said Powershare Home Backup was live for new Model 3 and Model Y owners with a Powerwall 3, extending home backup "by over 2 days," and Tesla's main account summed it up as "With Powerwall 3, every Tesla can now serve as your home’s backup battery." On Wednesday afternoon Tesla clarified who qualifies: "Available for all Cybertrucks & new Model 3/Y ordered on or after Oct 1 this year."

Superchargers and storm prep

New Supercharger sites opened in Miami (12 stalls), Letterkenny, Ireland (8), Czeladź, Poland (6) and Bucharest, Romania (12), Tesla Charging posted. Ahead of Hurricane Isaias, Tesla Charging advised owners charging at home to "charge to 100%".

Quick Hit

Wes Morrill @wmorrill3, Cybertruck lead engineer, quoted an owner who switched from a Ford and said he saved $650 on gas last month: "Several Cybertruck owners in this thread sharing the same sentiment."

IV · Chips, Robots & the Roadster

"We will build and run the fab"

Elon Musk shut down talk that TSMC would operate Terafab, Intel said it is staying in, and a new patent filing hints at Optimus's sense of touch.

Elon Musk on Terafab @elonmusk · CEO, Tesla and SpaceX

Terafab is the chip-making venture of Tesla, SpaceX and xAI. Tesla investor Herbert Ong (@herbertong) had posted that Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC) was exploring a role in the project. At 2:33 a.m. EDT on Wednesday, Musk answered:

"No, we will build and run the fab. Let there be ZERO doubt about that. Maybe TSMC subleases part of the Terafab if they want, but nothing more than that."
Elon Musk · @elonmusk · post, Wed 2:33 a.m. EDT

Later Wednesday, Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan told Bloomberg in Tokyo that Intel will stay involved; Terafab plans to use Intel's 14A manufacturing process, Tom's Hardware reported.

Quick Hit

Dan Ives, starting coverage at his new firm, Yorkville Ives: "While automotive remains the company's foundation, we believe the next phase of the Tesla story will be defined by monetizing software, autonomy, and physical AI across an installed base few global technology companies can replicate." (via Yahoo Finance)

Optimus: a patent for touch

Tesla will also bring an Optimus Gen 2 robot to the Paris Motor Show next week (see Section I).

"A new Tesla patent application has been published publicly today for a 3D tactile touch sensor for a humanoid robot. These sensors could allow Optimus to detect touch, pressure, and force across different parts of its hands, improving its ability to grip and manipulate objects."
Sawyer Merritt · @SawyerMerritt · post, Thu 7:33 a.m. EDT

Roadster: one week out

The next-generation Roadster unveiling is now set for Thursday, October 15. Tesla moved it from October 1 because severe weather threatened an outdoor demonstration, according to TeslaNorth and Paul Tan. Start time and livestream details had not been announced as of this writing.
