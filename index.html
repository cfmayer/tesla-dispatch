The Morning Picture

A record 285 Cybercabs on Thanksgiving push Tesla past 1,000 in Texas

Tesla registered a record 285 Cybercabs with Texas on Thanksgiving, taking its count of the steering-wheel-free two-seaters past 1,000 for the first time, as FSD v15 took over the top of Tesla's US homepage and showroom displays. Elon Musk agreed with an owner who called a storm drive on FSD 15.0.1 "flawless" and thanked Tesla's customers for the holiday.

A new software update, 2026.44.3.1, lets drivers warm the battery manually before a fast charge and adds Automatic Collision Evasion on Hardware 4 cars, and Tesla Charging posted six new Supercharger sites across Asia and Australia. Drone footage showed Giga Press foundations at Giga Texas's Optimus factory. Markets were closed Thursday; TSLA's last close was $380.14 on Wednesday, and today's session ends early, at 1:00 p.m. EST.

I · Full Self-Driving (FSD)

1,107 Cybercabs on the Texas roster

The biggest one-day jump yet puts Cybercabs at 60% of Tesla's Texas Robotaxi fleet; v15 moves to the front of Tesla's website.

"Tesla just registered a record 285 Cybercabs for its Robotaxi fleet, bringing the total number registered to date to 1,107, crossing the 1,000 mark for the first time, according to the Texas DMV," Sawyer Merritt posted at 2:38 p.m. EST Thursday. The Texas Department of Motor Vehicles listed 1,841 vehicles under Tesla Robotaxi LLC on Nov. 26, 1,107 Cybercabs and 734 Model Ys, so Cybercabs now make up about 60% of the roster, up from about 53% at its previous check, TeslaNorth reported; the FSD Database said the latest batch was 2027-model-year cars.

The figures count registrations, not cars giving rides, Not a Tesla App noted. Tesla's last official update, in October, said more than 300 Cybercabs were running in Austin; Cybercabs carry riders only in Austin and Dallas so far. Waymo has 1,409 vehicles registered in Texas, Nic Cruz Patane posted at 3:03 p.m. EST. By the count of Robotaxi Tracker, an independent site that reads the state register, Tesla now leads Waymo by 432, and every one of Tesla's 334 net additions since Nov. 17 has been a Cybercab, while Waymo added 88, Servo Space reported.

v15 takes the homepage

"The homepage of Tesla's website in the U.S. has been updated to feature FSD V15," Merritt posted at 1:32 p.m. EST Thursday with a screenshot, and Patane posted at 8:53 p.m. EST a photo of a Tesla showroom advertising FSD V15. Tesla's own account wished owners a happy "Show your family FSD" day at 3:01 p.m. EST and asked them to share their relatives' reactions.

"Utterly boring, and… perfect"

An owner who posts as congressdj wrote at 9:39 p.m. EST that a 30-minute drive in a storm on FSD 15.0.1 "was a boring drive. Utterly boring, and… perfect. Nothing happened. The drive was flawless." Musk replied "Yup" at 12:26 a.m. EST Friday. Version 15.0.1 began reaching a small number of Model Ys with Hardware 4 (HW4) on Nov. 21, FSD Update reported.

What v15 changes

Tesla's release notes for v15.0.1 say it "upgrades every part of the end-to-end driving network," including a vision encoder shared between FSD, Robotaxi and Optimus that is "trained on millions of internet videos" and improves "the critical intervention rate by 25x." Tesla Reality View replaces the old driving visualization with "a view from an arbitrary virtual camera situated above your vehicle." The build also lets FSD slow for speed cameras, which a driver can cancel twice to save the location, and adds a Bike Rack Mode in which FSD "pulls over near your destination and does not reverse."

Automatic Collision Evasion

Software update 2026.44.3.1, which Not a Tesla App dates to Nov. 26, adds Automatic Collision Evasion on HW4 cars, according to the release notes: it "activates Full Self-Driving (Supervised) to try to keep your vehicle safe and then continue driving" when the car detects that a driver steering manually is not paying enough attention, for example "reaching toward the back seat," or that FSD may have been disengaged by accident. The build also carries FSD (Supervised) v14.3 Lite for Hardware 3 cars.

Quick Hit

Stickers for the app. Tesla has begun putting "Download Tesla Robotaxi App" stickers on the back of Cybercabs in Dallas, Nic Cruz Patane posted at 1:02 p.m. EST Thursday with a photo.

II · Energy & Charging

Warm the battery yourself

Manual preconditioning reaches cars worldwide in 2026.44.3.1; six Supercharger openings from Singapore to New South Wales.

"You can now manually precondition your battery to reach its optimal temperature before fast charging to save time," Tesla's notes for 2026.44.3.1 say, as archived by Not a Tesla App. Owners are told to start about an hour before they plan to charge, and the car holds the temperature for 30 minutes; preconditioning can also be started from the Tesla app (Charging > Precondition for Fast Charging, app v4.63.0). The feature is marked available worldwide, alongside a Charger Network View that clusters charger pins on the map.

Until now a Tesla usually warmed its battery only when navigating to a fast charger, which on a short winter drive may not leave enough time, NotATeslaKiller wrote; it said the rollout has barely started, with early installs in Austria and Poland, and that the holiday software package is still expected separately, later in December. In the TeslaFi sample only two of about 12,300 tracked cars had it on Nov. 26–27, the site said. Teslascope spotted the build at 1:53 a.m. EST Friday on a 2019 Model 3 in Évora, Portugal.

Six new Supercharger sites

Tesla Charging posted six openings overnight: Bugis+ in Singapore, 4 stalls (12:08 a.m. EST); both sides of the Deokpyeong Rest Area in South Korea, 8 stalls toward Gangneung (12:10 a.m.) and 8 toward Incheon (12:10 a.m.); Griffin Street in Heatherbrae, New South Wales, Australia, 12 stalls (12:12 a.m.); Oaza Niiji in Yamatocho, Saga, Japan, 4 stalls (2:02 a.m.); and The Circle Ratchapruk in Bangkok, Thailand, 4 stalls (2:20 a.m.).

III · Optimus & Factories

Giga Press foundations for Optimus

Drone footage shows casting-machine mounts going into the robot factory at Giga Texas.

Drone footage of Giga Texas flown on Wednesday by Joe Tegtmeyer shows four foundations being built on the northeast side of the Austin plant for the Optimus humanoid-robot factory, with Giga Press machine-frame mounts already installed, Tesla Oracle reported. The foundations are reportedly for presses from IDRA, the Italian company whose Giga Presses Tesla shipped to Austin for Cybertruck production. The Austin site will be Optimus's second production facility, after Fremont, California.

Musk said on the third-quarter earnings call, as quoted by the site: "We've started early production. The growth of Optimus is an S-curve, but the initial curve is a shallow slope. We have the vast majority of the 1 million unit/year line installed at Fremont."

Quick Hit

Thanks to customers. "Super appreciate your support. Tesla wouldn't be where it is without our awesome customers," Musk wrote at 4:23 p.m. EST Thursday, quoting Tesla's Thanksgiving post.

A second letter, headed "Time Is Running Out," says "Tesla's next chapter starts with your vote." For most shareholders the deadline is 11:59 p.m. EST on Wednesday, Dec. 2; the meeting is Thursday, Dec. 3.
